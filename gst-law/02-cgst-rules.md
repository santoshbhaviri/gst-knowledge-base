# CGST Rules, 2017

**Last updated:** 12 Oct 2026

**About this file**
- The CGST Rules were notified by Notification 3/2017-Central Tax (19.06.2017) and have been amended many times since. The States adopt the same rules as SGST Rules.
- **Full current text:** [text/cgst-rules.md](text/cgst-rules.md), taken rule by rule from the [CBIC portal](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content-page/explore-rules/1000006) on 12 Oct 2026. It includes the Fifth Amendment Rules, 2025 (rule 31D). IGST Rules: [text/igst-rules.md](text/igst-rules.md).
- **(verified)** below means checked against that text or the notification PDF on CBIC.

## Rule changes coming from the 57th Council (not yet notified as of 12.10.2026)
- **Registration:** 14B (e-commerce sellers), 19 (auto-amendment), 21, 21A, 22 and 23A (system-based cancellation).
- **Returns:** 60(6A) (IMS), 61(1A) and 61(1B) (GSTR-3B corrections), 86C and 86D (new statements).
- **Refunds and credit blocking:** 89(4)(C) cap removed, 86A objection right.
- **Rule 96(10):** omitted with effect from 23.10.2017, following the Supreme Court.

See [05-gst-council-meetings.md](05-gst-council-meetings.md).

## Rules most relevant to data and analytics work
- **Rule 48(4) e-invoice and rules 138 to 138F e-way bill:** transaction-level, near-real-time records of goods movement. These are the core data for supply-chain and logistics analysis.
- **Rules 59 to 61 (GSTR-1, GSTR-2B, GSTR-3B):** the invoice graph between suppliers and recipients, used to detect fake ITC.
- **Rules 88C and 88D:** existing system-driven mismatch rules, a model for analytics-led enforcement.
- **Rule 86A:** credit blocking driven by risk analytics.
