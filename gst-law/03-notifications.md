## 1. Notification series (how CBIC numbers them)

| Series | Issued under | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Central Tax (CT) | CGST Act and Rules | Procedure: due dates, rule amendments, commencement of sections, officers, waivers |
| Central Tax (Rate) (CTR) | s.9, s.11, s.15(5) CGST | Rates, exemptions, reverse charge |
| Integrated Tax (IT) and Integrated Tax (Rate) (ITR) | IGST Act | Same, for IGST |
| Union Territory Tax and UTT (Rate) | UTGST Act | Same, for UTGST |
| Compensation Cess and Cess (Rate) | Compensation Act | Cess rates |
| State Tax / State Tax (Rate) | Each SGST Act | States' matching notifications, with their own numbering |

## 3. Landmark procedural (Central Tax) notifications by topic

The numbers and dates below were checked against the CBIC list; the effect described is a summary.

- **Officers and jurisdiction:** 02/2017-CT (jurisdiction of Central Tax officers), amended by 10/2025-CT (13.03.2025).
- **Composition:** 14/2019-CT (07.03.2019, limit ₹1.5 cr); 02/2019-CTR (07.03.2019, 6% scheme for service suppliers up to ₹50 lakh).
- **E-invoicing (rule 48(4)) thresholds:**
  - 13/2020-CT (21.03.2020) and later amendments brought the threshold down in steps: ₹500 cr (1.10.2020), ₹100 cr (1.1.2021), ₹50 cr (1.4.2021), ₹20 cr (1.4.2022), ₹10 cr (1.10.2022).
  - **₹5 cr from 1.8.2023** (10/2023-CT, 10.05.2023).
  - The 57th Council recommends extending e-invoicing to RCM supplies from unregistered persons and to imported services.
- **QRMP scheme:** 84/2020-CT and 85/2020-CT (10.11.2020; scheme from 1.1.2021).
- **Time limits for demands (s.73(10)):** extended for early financial years under s.168A by 13/2022-CT (05.07.2022), 09/2023-CT (31.03.2023) and 56/2023-CT (28.12.2023).
- **Amnesty schemes (2021, 2023):** late fee waivers and revocation of cancelled registrations, notified as a series of CT notifications.
- **Section 128A waiver:** rule 164 added by 20/2024-CT (08.10.2024); date under s.128A(1) notified by 21/2024-CT (08.10.2024); Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024 provisions brought in by 17/2024-CT (27.09.2024). Clarified by Circular 248/05/2025-GST.

**Nothing after 02/2026-CT is listed on CBIC as of 12 Oct 2026.** The portal's newest GST notifications are 02/2026-CT (7 May 2026, uploaded 10 June 2026) and 01/2026-CTR, ITR and UTTR (30 April 2026). So, per the official portal:
- No commencement notification has been issued yet for ss.153 to 155 of the Finance Act, 2026 (ss.15(3), 34 and 54). The CBIC Act text marks them "w.e.f. yet to be notified".
- No CGST rule amendment has been notified in 2026.
- Press reports of 2026 changes (GSTAT appeal deadline extended to 31.07.2026; e-way bill "Ship-to GSTIN" from 1.8.2026) are not backed by any notification on CBIC. They may be GSTN or GSTAT orders and advisories rather than CBIC notifications.

The portal sometimes uploads weeks after issue (02/2026-CT took a month), so recent items may still appear. The Gazette (egazette.gov.in) would be the check, but it cannot be reached from here: see [06-sources-and-gaps.md](06-sources-and-gaps.md).
