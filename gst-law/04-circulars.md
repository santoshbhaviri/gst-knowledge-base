# GST circulars and instructions: by topic, and the latest list

**Last updated:** 12 Oct 2026

**About this file**
- Circulars are issued under section 168 of the CGST Act. They bind officers, not taxpayers or courts.
- CBIC has issued 256 CGST circulars to date (12.10.2026), numbered serially. The format is *serial/serial-within-year/year-GST*, for example 251/08/2025-GST.
- **Source: the CBIC Tax Information Portal, GST > Circulars, downloaded 12 Oct 2026.** It lists CGST circulars 1 to 256 with no gaps, and nothing after 256/02/2026-GST (25.07.2026).
- **Full list of all 272 circulars:** [04a-circulars-full-list.md](04a-circulars-full-list.md), with a CBIC link for each. Machine-readable: [data/circulars.csv](data/circulars.csv). Instructions: [data/instructions.csv](data/instructions.csv). Orders: [data/orders.csv](data/orders.csv).

## 2. Landmark circulars by topic

Numbers and dates checked against the CBIC list (12 Oct 2026).

**Input tax credit**
- 183/15/2022-GST (27.12.2022): GSTR-2A vs GSTR-3B mismatch for FY 2017-18 and 2018-19.
- 193/05/2023-GST (17.07.2023): the same issue for 1.4.2019 to 31.12.2021.
- 211/05/2024-GST (26.06.2024): s.16(4) time limit for RCM supplies from unregistered persons.
- 170/02/2022-GST (06.07.2022): reporting inter-State supplies and ITC reversals correctly in GSTR-3B.

**Refunds**
- 125/44/2019-GST (18.11.2019): master circular on refunds, cited in the 57th Council FAQs.
- 135/05/2020-GST (31.03.2020): refund-related issues.

**E-way bill and detention**
- 41/15/2018-GST (13.04.2018): procedure for interception, detention and confiscation (MOV forms).
- 64/38/2018-GST (14.09.2018): minor discrepancies in e-way bills.

**Fake invoices and ITC fraud**
- 171/03/2022-GST (06.07.2022): demand and penalty provisions for fake invoices without supply.

**Related parties and valuation**
- 199/11/2023-GST (17.07.2023): services between offices of the same organisation in different States (ISD vs cross-charge).
- 204/16/2023-GST (27.10.2023): corporate guarantees.
- 210/04/2024-GST (26.06.2024): valuation of **imported** services from a related person where the recipient is eligible for full ITC.

**Litigation management**
- 207/1/2024-GST (26.06.2024): monetary limits for department appeals: GSTAT ₹20 lakh, High Court ₹1 cr, Supreme Court ₹2 cr.

**Proper officers**
- 3/3/2017-GST (05.07.2017): proper officers other than for registration and composition.
- 31/05/2018-GST (09.02.2018), proper officers under ss.73 and 74, as amended by 239/33/2024-GST (04.12.2024).

**Investigation instructions**
- Instruction 01/2022-23 (GST-Investigation): no coercive recovery during search.
- Instruction 02/2022-23 (GST-Investigation) (17.08.2022): guidelines on arrest and bail, **since reissued as Instruction 01/2025-GST (13.01.2025)**.
- Instruction 03/2022-23 (17.08.2022): summons under s.70. Instruction 04/2022-23 (01.09.2022): launching prosecution.

These are especially relevant now that the 57th Council has proposed omitting the power of arrest.

## 3. What is missing
- **Taxpayer-facing GSTN advisories** (e-way bill, IMS, e-invoice) are on gst.gov.in, not CBIC. They are not covered here.
- **State circulars** are not covered here.
- Circular texts have not been read one by one; the subjects in the full list are CBIC's own one-line descriptions. Any circular can be opened from its link.
