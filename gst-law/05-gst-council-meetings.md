## 57th meeting: 8 October 2026, New Delhi (verified)

**Sources**
- PIB press release, 8 Oct 2026: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2320934
- Council copy of the release: https://www.gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-10/press_release.pdf
- Council FAQs: https://www.gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-10/faqs_on_recommendations_made_in_57th_gst_council_meeting.pdf

The meeting was chaired by the Union Finance Minister. The 56th meeting dealt with rates; this one dealt mainly with **process reform, ease of doing business and clarifications**. The release says the recommendations take legal effect only once they are implemented through notifications, circulars or amendments to the Act. **As of 12 Oct 2026, none of the implementing notifications or amendments had been found; still none on a re-check on 17 Oct 2026.** CBIC held outreach meetings with trade on these reforms on 15-16 Oct 2026 (Hyderabad, Chennai, Bengaluru) and sought feedback on a proposed centralised GST administration for taxpayers under Central jurisdiction (a proposal only).

### A. Process reforms
**Registration**
- A circular and FAQs will list the documents required for registration. REG-01 will get drop-boxes for the prescribed documents.
- Rule 19: amendments to registration particulars will be auto-accepted, except a change of principal place of business (PPoB). Taxpayers registered under rule 14A (the automatic route) also get PPoB changes auto-accepted.
- Cancellation, Phase 1: a REG-16 application is auto-accepted once returns are filed and dues are paid. This applies if ITC passed on never exceeded ₹2.5 lakh in any month, or if it did but the final return GSTR-10 is filed on time.
- Cancellation, Phase 2: all cancellation applications are auto-accepted on the same conditions, and GSTR-10 details can be entered within REG-16.
- Suo motu cancellation: some grounds in rule 21 will be removed. Rules 21A and 22 and a new rule 23A will provide system-based cancellation and revocation for non-filing of returns or non-furnishing of bank details.
- New rule 14B: automatic registration for small e-commerce sellers with no physical presence in a State, where ITC passed on is up to ₹2.5 lakh a month and the e-commerce operator's warehouse is declared as PPoB.

**Returns.** These changes apply from the return for **April 2027**, after a public consultation.
- GSTR-1, GSTR-1A and IFF will be enhanced to reconcile better with GSTR-3B.
- New rule 86D: an electronic statement of RCM tax paid and ITC claimed.
- New rule 86C: an electronic statement of credit reversals and reclaims.
- Rule 61(1A) and 61(1B): GSTR-3B liability can be corrected to match GSTR-1, and GSTR-3B ITC to match GSTR-2B.
- Rule 60(6A): IMS gets accept, reject and pending options, with a time limit on how long a credit note can stay pending.
- DRC-03 will capture the invoice against which a payment is made.

**Refunds**
- Phase 1:
  - Refunds of excess balance in the electronic cash ledger are fully auto-sanctioned.
  - The time for an acknowledgement or deficiency memo drops from 15 days to 10, with deemed acknowledgement after that.
  - For zero-rated and inverted-duty claims, 90% is sanctioned provisionally and automatically, based on system risk scoring.
- Phase 2: automated acknowledgement, and full automated sanction of zero-rated claims after adjusting dues.
- RFD-01 will capture data in a machine-readable format. Scanned documents are no longer needed for zero-rated and inverted-duty claims.
- Rule 89(4)(C): the cap of 1.5 times the value of like domestic goods is removed.
- Section 54(14): the ₹1,000 minimum applies to the combined refund across CGST, SGST/UTGST and IGST.
- Section 115 becomes a standalone provision on interest on refund of pre-deposit.

**Disputes**
- Sections 73, 74 and 74A:
  - No show cause notice where tax (CGST + SGST + IGST + cess) is below ₹10,000. Pending cases below this are to be closed on the same basis.
  - Penalty is deemed concluded when tax, interest and penalty are paid voluntarily.
  - In non-fraud cases, the penalty falls to 5% if tax and interest are paid within 30 days of the order under section 73, or 60 days under section 74A.
  - The ₹10,000 minimum penalty in non-fraud cases is removed.
- Section 125: the general penalty maximum falls from ₹25,000 to ₹10,000.
- Sections 107(6) and 112(8): for penalty-only appeals, pre-deposit is capped at ₹40 crore (₹20 crore CGST plus ₹20 crore SGST/UTGST).
- A circular will guide officers on notices, orders, invoking fraud or suppression, and natural justice, including personal hearings.

### B. Other major reforms
- **ITC refunds:**
  - Inverted-duty refunds will include ITC on input services, for ITC availed on or after **1 Nov 2026**.
  - ITC on capital goods becomes refundable for zero-rated and inverted-duty supplies, spread over 60 months, for ITC availed on or after **1 Apr 2027**.
- **Section 17(5) blocks removed** for outdoor catering, health and life insurance, telecom towers, pipelines laid outside the factory, free samples, and goods destroyed on shelf-life expiry where the law requires it.
- **Exports (IGST Act):**
  - Section 2(6)(v) is omitted, so exports between distinct persons can qualify.
  - Section 13(3)(a) is omitted.
  - An explanation is added to section 16(1) on delivery to an SEZ or FTWZ against payment from an overseas buyer.
  - A circular will clarify acceptable payment in foreign exchange or rupees.
- **Arrest and prosecution:**
  - **Section 69 (arrest) is omitted.**
  - The prosecution threshold rises from ₹1 crore to **₹5 crore**.
  - Section 132 is narrowed: clause (i) is omitted, and clause (c) is limited to fraudulent ITC without receipt of goods or services, or without an invoice.
- **E-way bill (sections 68, 129, 130):**
  - Interception only on specific intelligence, authorised by an officer of at least Joint Commissioner rank.
  - Inspection only where the supplier or recipient is registered or located in the intercepting State, so no interception in transit States.
  - Goods with no e-way bill or document can be checked anywhere.
  - No confiscation under section 130 of goods or conveyances in transit.
- **Other changes:**
  - Transfer of IPR title, temporary or permanent, is a supply of services (Schedule II).
  - Rule 86A: a right to object to blocked credit and to a hearing.
  - Late fee waiver under section 39(1) for turnover up to ₹5 crore if the return is filed by the end of the due month.
  - ARQP (optional annual return with quarterly payment) approved in principle for B2C-only taxpayers with turnover up to ₹5 crore.
  - E-invoicing extended to RCM supplies from unregistered persons and to imported services, for taxpayers with turnover of ₹5 crore or more.
  - GSTAT provisions aligned with the Tribunals Reforms Act, 2026.

### C. Rates and classification
**Goods**
- Sublimation paper falls under heading 4809.
- All toys under heading 9503 are covered by the 09/2025-CTR entries.
- Seaweed-extract bio-stimulants fall under heading 3101 as fertiliser.
- Psyllium (isabgol) seed is at Nil.
- Re-treaded tractor tyres are taxed at the same rate as new tractor tyres.
- Margin-scheme dealers in used vehicles can claim ITC on inputs other than the vehicles themselves.
- RCM on plastic scrap, e-waste, tyre scrap and used cooking oil supplied by unregistered persons, plus **2% TDS on B2B supplies** of these items.
- Compensation cess regularised for supplies to the CSD and Unit Run Canteens.

**Services**
- Optional 5% (with restricted ITC) for EV passenger transport where charging is included.
- 5% without ITC for ECO-routed delivery services.
- Same-line ITC allowed for restaurants, hotels up to ₹7,500 per unit per day, and gyms.
- Exemptions:
  - Helicopter seat-sharing in the North-East, Sikkim and Bagdogra
  - Storage of sowing seeds
  - Coffee curing
  - Seamen's Provident Fund Organisation services
  - Import of services from a related foreign shipping line without consideration
  - NHAI toll-operate-transfer (TOT) concessions
- R&D exemption under Entry 44A to work on self-certification by the head of the institution.
- Funds Transfer Pricing notional interest counts as "interest".

## 56th meeting: 3 September 2025, New Delhi (verified; the meeting notice gives 3–4 Sep 2025)

**Sources**
- Press release: https://gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/press_release_press_information_bureau.pdf
- FAQs: https://gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/faq.pdf
- Meeting notice: https://www.gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-08/meeting_notice_56th_gst_council_meeting_0.pdf

This is the "next-generation GST" (GST 2.0) rate rationalisation.
- **The rate structure became two main rates, 5% (merit) and 18% (standard), plus a 40% de-merit rate** for luxury and sin goods. This replaced the 5/12/18/28 slabs.
- It took effect on **22 Sep 2025**, through Notifications 09/2025-CTR and 10/2025-CTR, both dated 17.09.2025. These replaced the base schedules 01/2017-CTR and 02/2017-CTR.
- Tobacco and pan masala stayed on the old rates plus compensation cess until the cess loans were repaid. That transition happened on **1 Feb 2026**:
  - These goods moved to 40% GST (bidis to 18%).
  - Compensation cess on them ended.
  - The Central Excise (Amendment) Act, 2025 and the Health Security se National Security Cess Act, 2025 took over the extra levy.
  - Source for the 1 Feb 2026 change is press coverage of the 31 Dec 2025 notifications: [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/excise-duty-on-tobacco-health-cess-on-pan-masala-from-february-1-126010101013_1.html). The official CBIC notification text was not reached.
- Examples of rate changes:
  - To Nil: UHT milk, paneer, Indian breads, 33 lifesaving drugs.
  - To 5%: many packaged foods, soaps, shampoo, toothpaste, bicycles, tractors, medicines, man-made fibre and yarn, renewable-energy devices, hotel rooms up to ₹7,500, gyms and salons.
  - To 18% (from 28%): ACs, TVs, cement, small cars, motorcycles up to 350cc, and buses, trucks and three-wheelers. All auto parts now have a uniform 18%.
- RSP-based valuation for tobacco and pan masala is notified under section 15(5) (Notification 19/2025-CT, 31.12.2025).
- **GSTAT:**
  - To take appeals before end-September 2025 and start hearings by end-December 2025.
  - 30.06.2026 is the last date for backlog appeals. Press coverage reports that this was later extended to 31.07.2026.
  - The Principal Bench also acts as the National Appellate Authority for Advance Ruling.
- Inverted-duty refunds get 90% provisional sanction administratively, using the risk-based model already used for zero-rated refunds.

## 55th meeting: 21 December 2024, Jaisalmer (verified)

**Source:** PIB https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2086873

**Rates**
- Fortified rice kernel 5%.
- Gene therapy exempt.
- Used vehicles: 18% on the margin.
- AAC blocks with more than 50% fly ash: 12%.
- Popcorn classification clarified.
- "Pre-packaged and labelled" means retail packs up to 25 kg or 25 litres.
- Penal charges by banks and NBFCs are not taxable.

**Law**
- Vouchers are neither goods nor services; sections 12(4), 13(4) and rule 32(6) are to be omitted.
- Schedule III para 8(aa) covers SEZ/FTWZ warehoused goods, retrospective from 1.7.2017.
- New section 148A for **track-and-trace** of evasion-prone goods.
- Section 17(5)(d) wording changed from "plant or machinery" to "plant and machinery", retrospective.
- Pre-deposit for penalty-only appeals is 10%.
- ISD to cover inter-state RCM.
- Rule 16A temporary ID.
- IMS-based GSTR-2B.

**Other:** the GoM on compensation cess had its deadline extended to 30.06.2025.

## 1st to 54th meetings

**Checked on 12 Oct 2026 against the signed minutes of every meeting from 1 to 54** (downloaded from gstcouncil.gov.in; the 52nd minutes are a scanned PDF and were read page by page). Each decision carries its paragraph number, or the page where the scanned text loses the paragraph number. Dates and venues are as stated in the minutes. The minutes of meeting N are adopted at meeting N+1, so they are the final record.

**Errors found in the earlier summaries and corrected here**
- **1st:** the 14% compensation growth rate was not decided here; it was left open (para 38) and fixed at the 3rd meeting (para 34).
- **6th and 8th:** "dual control" was not decided; it was deferred at the 8th (para 30) and settled at the 9th (para 28). The 9th also moved rollout to 1 July 2017 (para 30).
- **13th:** four sets of Rules were approved only in principle (para 7), not finally.
- **17th:** e-way bill rules were not approved; only an interim one-line rule, with States keeping their own systems (para 5.4.5). They were approved at the 20th (para 11).
- **18th:** "GST went live" was background, not a decision; the 1 July date was fixed at the 15th (para 22.7).
- **23rd:** the "178 items cut" figure is not in the minutes; they record the 28% slab being pruned to about 62 headings (para 16.2).
- **32nd:** States could choose a ₹20 lakh or ₹40 lakh threshold for goods (para 11.4), not a flat ₹40 lakh.
- **38th:** the 21-7 vote decided only that the lottery rate would be uniform; the 28% figure was then agreed by consensus (paras 6.20, 7). The minutes do not call it the first vote.
- **41st:** no decision; States were given seven working days to choose a borrowing option (paras 63, 65).
- **52nd:** States were not allowed to tax ENA; ENA for potable liquor was kept out of GST and the question of State VAT on it was left undecided (para 5.37).
- **54th:** the 18%→12% cut was for extruded savoury snacks, not namkeen (already 12%); insurance went to the existing rate-rationalisation GoM, not a new GoM (para 5.148).
- **Venues and dates:** 40th was by video conference (not New Delhi); 43rd was "at New Delhi" (not VC); 41st and 35th minutes state no venue; 33rd met by VC on 20 Feb and at Vigyan Bhawan on 24 Feb; 47th ran 28–29 June 2022.
