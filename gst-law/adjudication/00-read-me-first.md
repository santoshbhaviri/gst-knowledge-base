**Live deadlines (as on 14.10.2026):**
- s.73, FY 2022-23: new SCNs are already time-barred (last date 30.09.2026); pending SCNs must be decided by **31.12.2026**.
- s.74, FY 2020-21: decide by **28.02.2027**.
- FY 2024-25 onwards falls under s.74A.
- Where an order relies on Notifications 9/2023 or 56/2023, note that SLP(C) 4240/2025 is pending in the Supreme Court. The Telangana HC (*Brunda Infra*, 02.01.2025) did not uphold them: it decided on the Covid exclusion and observed that a Council recommendation is a sine qua non.

**Changed on 14.10.2026 after the case law was checked against the judgments:**
- **Signature and DIN/RFN:** *Gudla Vanaja* (Telangana HC Full Bench, 30.09.2026) overruled *Bigleap* and supersedes *Silver Oak Villas*. An SCN or order without a DIN/RFN is invalid; one electronically authenticated on the portal is valid without a visible signature. Digital signing remains good practice.
- **Multi-year SCNs:** *Goldstone Infra* (Telangana HC DB, 27.01.2026) upheld a composite s.74 SCN and order. One SCN per year is still recommended as safer practice, but it is not a legal requirement in Telangana.
- **Hearing:** *B.K. Steels* was a consent order; cite s.75(4) itself as the primary authority.
