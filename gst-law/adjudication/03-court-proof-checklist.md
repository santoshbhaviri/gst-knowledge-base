# Court-proof checklist: why GST notices and orders get struck down, and how to draft so they don't

**For:** Assistant Commissioner, CGST, Sangareddy Division (Medchal Commissionerate, Telangana).
**Compiled:** 14 Oct 2026.
**Built from:** case-law files 01 to 09 in `/mnt/project-files/gst-law/case-law/` (the compendium repeats them). Section and rule numbers were checked against `text/cgst-act.md` and `text/cgst-rules.md` (CBIC text, downloaded 12 Oct 2026). Three CBIC documents were read in full from the CBIC portal: Instruction 04/2023-GST, Instruction 01/2024-GST and Circulars 224/18/2024-GST and 249/06/2025-GST.

> **Reliability warning. Every case entry below is "Secondary".** Each one was built from web-search summaries, because court websites were blocked. None of the judgments has been read in full. Dates, case numbers and party names marked "not found" were not found. **Read the judgment before you quote it in an SCN or an order.** The statutory text quoted here is from the Act and Rules, which are primary sources.

**How to read each entry**
- **Defect:** what went wrong, in one line.
- **Cases:** the court, the case name and how far it binds, with the case-law file it comes from, e.g. [03] = `03-demands-adjudication-appeals.md`.
- **Binding:** **SC** = Supreme Court, binding on everyone (Art. 141). **BINDING IN TELANGANA** = Telangana High Court. **Persuasive** = other High Courts. **GSTAT** = the Tribunal; authorities below it follow it, but a High Court or SC ruling prevails over it.
- **Result:** what courts did: **quashed**, **remanded** (sent back for a fresh order), **relegated** (sent to the appeal remedy, no decision on the defect), or **relief refused** (no prejudice shown).
- **Fix:** exactly what to write or do so the defect cannot arise.

**A pattern specific to Telangana.** The Telangana HC often does not quash. It sends the taxpayer to appeal with a direction to condone delay or to hear the case (*Bollant Industries*, *Sri Sai Ram Traders*, *SBC Chem*, *Sahasra Enterprises*, *Inam Impex*). That is not an endorsement of the order. The same defect will be tested in appeal and before the GSTAT Hyderabad Bench, which has been sitting since 20 Apr 2026 [09].

## Part 2. The grounds, stage by stage

### Stage A. Before the SCN

**A1. Parallel proceedings: the State has already started proceedings on the same subject matter**
- **Defect:** CGST SCN issued where a State tax officer had already initiated proceedings on the same subject matter, or vice versa (s.6(2)(b)).
- **Cases:**
  - *Armour Security (India) Ltd v. Commissioner, CGST Delhi East*, SC, 14.08.2025, SLP (C) 6092/2025. **SC.** A summons, search or seizure is not "initiation of proceedings"; an SCN is. Guidelines say action from audit or detailed scrutiny is for the administration to which the taxpayer is assigned. Bench reported inconsistently. [08]
  - Telangana HC, W.P. "3204 of 2025" (party not found): the Central authority acted first, so the State order was quashed and the State told to transfer its material. **BINDING IN TELANGANA if confirmed.** [08]
  - *United Bakers Cooperative Society v. AC Central Tax*, 23.04.2026 (court not confirmed as Telangana): same period but different issues is not the same subject matter; relegated. [08]
  - Delhi HC DGGI batch and *Sanskriti Exim*, Sept 2026: same assessee or overlapping period is not enough; identical liability is needed. Persuasive. [08]
- **Result:** quashed where the overlap is real; relief refused where the issues differ.
- **Fix:** before issue, check the portal for any SCN by the State on the same issue and period, and ask the State office. Add a paragraph to the SCN: *"It is verified from the common portal and by letter dated ___ to the [State office] that no proceedings on the subject matter of this notice (issue: ___; period: ___) have been initiated under the TGST Act."* If the issues differ, say how. Do not take up audit or scrutiny of a taxpayer assigned to the State (Armour guidelines).

**A2. Officer without jurisdiction ("proper officer")**
- **Defect:** SCN or order by an officer not assigned the function, or above or below the monetary limit set for that rank.
- **Cases:**
  - *NCS Pearson Inc. v. Joint Director, DGGI*, Karnataka HC, 16.07.2025: DGGI s.74 SCN quashed because the officer was not authorised. Persuasive; Karnataka HC reportedly not uniform. [08]
  - Contra: Madras HC 2024, Rajasthan HC, Delhi HC Sept 2026 uphold DGGI's power under Notification 14/2017-CT. Persuasive. [08]
- **Courts split.** No SC or Telangana ruling found.
- **Result:** quashed where authority is missing.
- **Fix:** in the opening paragraph of the SCN and the order, write: *"This notice/order is issued by the undersigned as proper officer under section ___, in terms of Notification No. ___ and Circular No. 31/05/2018-GST as amended [by Circulars 169/01/2022, 239/33/2024 / 254/11/2025 for s.74A], the amount involved being Rs ___."* For DGGI-issued SCNs adjudicated here, cite the notification or order making you the adjudicating authority. (The monetary limits in Circular 31/05/2018 were not re-read for this file; check the current text.)

**A3. SCN issued before the pre-notice reply period expired**
- **Defect:** DRC-01A or audit observations issued, then the SCN issued before the time given to reply had run.
- **Cases:** *Varian Medical Systems v. Union of India*, Delhi HC (DB), 2025, W.P.(C) 10642/2025: SCN issued one day before the reply period expired violated natural justice. Persuasive. [08]
- **Result:** quashed or remanded.
- **Law:** DRC-01A is optional ("may", rule 142(1A)). But once sent, wait.
- **Fix:** if you use DRC-01A, diarise the reply date and issue the SCN only after it. Record in the SCN: *"Intimation in DRC-01A dated ___ was issued; reply dated ___ [was / was not] received and is dealt with at para ___."*

**A4. Audit or special audit used as if it extended limitation**
- **Defect:** special audit ordered or SCN issued after the time to pass the s.73 order had expired.
- **Cases:** *Koenig Solutions v. AC, Audit Branch, GNCTD*, Delhi HC (DB), 09.07.2026, W.P.(C) 11410/2024: ADT-03 and DRC-01 set aside because the s.73 deadline had passed. Persuasive. [08]
- **Fix:** plan the audit so that the SCN meets s.73(2)/74(2) or s.74A(2), and the order meets s.73(10)/74(10)/74A(7). Audit is not a ground for exclusion under s.75(1) or (11).

**A5. The issue is already settled against the department, or the SCN rests on someone else's ruling**
- **Defect:** demand raised on a point that an SC ruling, a Telangana HC ruling, a statute or a binding circular has already settled; or the SCN relies on another taxpayer's advance ruling.
- **Cases:**
  - *Union of India v. Kamlakshi Finance*, SC (1991/1992; citation not confirmed): lower authorities must follow higher appellate orders and the jurisdictional HC. **SC.** [04]
  - *Ratan Melting & Wire*, SC Constitution Bench, 2008: circulars bind officers, but a circular contrary to an SC/HC ruling cannot be enforced. **SC.** [04]
  - Examples now settled: *Goodluck India*, SC, 06.08.2026 (rule 96(10) omission applies to all proceedings pending on 08.10.2024) [01]; s.16(5) for FY 2017-18 to 2020-21 (statute) [02]; leasehold assignment not a supply (*Gujarat Chamber*, Guj HC; Revenue SLP dismissed 21.07.2026, persuasive) [07]; hostel leasing exempt up to 17.07.2022 (*Taghar Vasudeva Ambrish*, **SC**, 04.12.2025) [07]; IGST on ocean freight in CIF imports (*Mohit Minerals*, **SC**) [04].
  - Advance ruling of another taxpayer: an HC (name not found) set aside an SCN that relied on it. s.103 makes an advance ruling binding only on the applicant and its jurisdictional officer. [04]
- **Result:** quashed.
- **Fix:** before drafting, search the case-law index for the issue. If a binding ruling goes against the demand, do not issue. If a ruling is under appeal without a stay, say so in the SCN.

**A6. "Voluntary" payment taken during a search**
- **Defect:** DRC-03 paid during a search or under pressure, later treated as an admission or adjusted against the demand.
- **Cases:** *Vallabh Textiles v. Senior Intelligence Officer*, Delhi HC, 20.12.2022, W.P.(C) 9834/2022: Rs 1.80 crore held not voluntary; refund with interest. Persuasive. *Bhumi Associate*, Gujarat HC, 2021 (interim): no recovery during search; voluntary DRC-03 only the next day. *Radhika Agarwal*, SC, 2025 reportedly deprecated coercion (that part not confirmed). Counter-trend: a coercion claim raised two years late was refused (court not identified). [04], [01]
- **Fix:** do not accept payment during a search. If a DRC-03 is filed later, record the date, issue DRC-04 (rule 142(2)) and keep a contemporaneous statement on voluntariness. In the SCN, give credit for the payment; do not call it an admission.

**A7. Writ against the SCN itself (department's defence)**
- Telangana HC does not usually entertain a writ at the SCN stage: *S. Shivraj Reddy Constructions v. AC (ST)*, **BINDING IN TELANGANA** [05]; also MP HC circular-trading case [02]. **Result:** relief refused. This protects a well-drafted SCN; it does not protect one with a jurisdiction, limitation or signature defect.

### Stage B. The SCN itself

**B1. Only a DRC-01 summary, no proper SCN**
- **Defect:** the noticee received only the DRC-01 summary, or a DRC-01 with a thin attachment, and no self-contained notice.
- **Law:** rule 142(1)(a): the summary in DRC-01 is served "along with" the notice. Instruction 04/2023-GST (23.11.2023, read in full) directs that the DRC-01 summary must be served electronically and the DRC-07 summary uploaded.
- **Cases:** *NKAS Services v. State of Jharkhand*, Jharkhand HC, 09.02.2022; *Construction Catalysers v. State of Assam*, Gauhati HC: summary cannot replace the SCN; order quashed with liberty for fresh proceedings. Persuasive. *Silver Oak Villas LLP*, Telangana HC (DB), 14.03.2024, WP 6671/2024 (unsigned summary attachment; SCN and order set aside) is **superseded** by the Full Bench in *Gudla Vanaja* (30.09.2026): do not cite it. No Telangana ruling squarely on "summary only" was found. [03], [05]
- **Result:** quashed; fresh proceedings allowed if within time.
- **Fix:** always issue a full SCN document (facts, provisions, computation, proposal, relied-upon documents) and upload it as the attachment to the DRC-01. Both are needed: the notice and the summary.

**B2. SCN, order or DRC-07 without a DIN/RFN, or unsigned** (legal basis changed 14.10.2026)
- **Defect:** SCN, order or DRC-07 issued without a DIN or RFN; or not digitally signed by the proper officer.
- **Law:** rule 26(3): notices, certificates and orders "shall be issued electronically by the proper officer ... through digital signature certificate or through E-signature ... or verified by any other mode ... notified by the Board". Rule 26(3) is worded for "this Chapter" (the registration chapter of the Rules). DIN/RFN: Circulars 122/41/2019, 128/47/2019 and 249/06/2025.
- **Cases:**
  - *Gudla Vanaja v. AC (ST), Saroor Nagar-I Circle*, Telangana HC (**Full Bench**), 30.09.2026, W.P. 20397/2025 batch (paras 154-155): SCNs and orders under Chapter XVIII electronically authenticated by the proper officer and uploaded on the portal are valid without a visible signature; upload is valid service under s.169(1)(d); limitation for appeal runs from the upload date; **a notice or order without a DIN or RFN is invalid.** **BINDING IN TELANGANA.** [03]
  - Overruled or superseded by it: *Bigleap Technologies v. State of Telangana* (DB, 28.02.2025, WP 21101/2024) and *Silver Oak Villas* (DB, 14.03.2024). The decisions that followed *Bigleap* (*Water Tech Engineers*, 2025; *Lakshmi Enterprises*, 02.02.2026) should not be relied on either. [03], [05]
- **Result:** in Telangana, a missing DIN/RFN is fatal; a missing visible signature alone is not.
- **Fix:** make sure every SCN, DRC-01/DRC-07 summary, hearing notice and order carries a DIN/RFN and is uploaded from your own authenticated login; record the upload date as the date of service. Signing the PDFs with your DSC remains good practice (other High Courts may still treat an unsigned document as defective).

**B3. Vague SCN: no facts, periods, figures or documents**
- **Defect:** the SCN repeats the words of the statute ("fraud, wilful misstatement or suppression", "contravention of the Act") without saying what was done, when, and on what evidence.
- **Cases:**
  - *CCE Bangalore v. Brindavan Beverages*, **SC**, 15.06.2007, CA 3417-3425/2002: the SCN is the foundation; vague allegations mean no real opportunity. [06]
  - *A1 Adil Traders v. Deputy State Tax Officer*, Telangana HC (DB), 04.03.2025, W.P. 4539/2025: the SCN (REG-17) gave no factual basis; reasons must be in the SCN itself, and the assessee is not expected to dig them out of an annexed internal departmental letter; extra reasons in the final order cannot cure it; SCN, suspension and order set aside. **BINDING IN TELANGANA.** *Rayees Metals v. Deputy STO*, Telangana HC: SCN copying s.29(2)(e) quashed. **BINDING IN TELANGANA.** [06]
  - *Aggarwal Dyeing*, Gujarat HC, 24.02.2022; Delhi HC W.P.(C) 10421/2023; *PAV Warehouse*, Madras HC 2024; *King Enterprises*, Karnataka HC 2026. Persuasive. [06]
  - *Augustan Textile Colours*, GSTAT Ernakulam, Sep 2026: a vague SCN vitiated the order. **GSTAT.** [09]
- **Counterweight:** *Trillion Lead Factory v. Superintendent of Central Tax*, Telangana HC, 02.02.2026, W.P. 537/2026: no relief where the annexed DGGI material made the charge clear and the taxpayer replied to it in detail (no prejudice). The two rulings pull in different directions on annexed documents, so do not rely on Trillion when drafting. **BINDING IN TELANGANA.** Also s.160(1): a proceeding is not invalid for a mistake, defect or omission if it is in substance in conformity with the Act. [05], [06]
- **Result:** quashed (often with liberty for a fresh SCN); relief refused where no prejudice is shown.
- **Fix:** for each issue write five things: (1) the facts found and how (e.g. "verification of GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B for Apr 2019 to Mar 2020"); (2) the provision breached, down to the clause; (3) the period; (4) the table of invoices, suppliers or figures; (5) the documents relied on (annexure list). Write these facts in the **body** of the SCN, not only in an annexed letter or report (*A1 Adil Traders*); annexures support the body, they do not replace it. Test: could the noticee reply point by point without asking you anything? In the order, record that the reply addressed each allegation (this shows no prejudice).

**B4. Section 74 (or 74A(5)(ii)) invoked without specific allegations**
- **Defect:** extended period or fraud penalty invoked on boilerplate, or on a mere mismatch, short payment or rate dispute.
- **Law:** s.74(1) and s.74A(5)(ii) require fraud, wilful misstatement or suppression "to evade tax". s.74 Explanation 2 defines "suppression" as non-declaration of information required in a return or document, or failure to furnish information asked for in writing.
- **Cases:**
  - *G.R. Infra Projects Ratlam v. State of M.P.*, **SC**, 19.08.2026, CA 11277/2026: the facts showing fraud or suppression must be in the SCN itself; a bland recital, especially using "or" between the grounds, is not enough; deficiencies cannot be cured by counter-affidavit. SCN quashed. [03]
  - *Sterling & Wilson v. Commissioner, Odisha*, **GSTAT Principal Bench**, 11.02.2026, APL/1/PB/2026: a GSTR-1/3B mismatch alone does not justify s.74. [09]
  - *Raghuvansh Agro Farms*, Allahabad HC 2025 (order lacked the ingredients; quashed); Madras HC Nov 2025 (both SCN and order must show the elements). Persuasive. [08], [03]
  - *Safecon Lifescience*, Allahabad HC; Revenue's SLP dismissed by SC 17.07.2026: s.74 cannot rest on another wing's unverified, undisclosed report. Persuasive (SLP dismissal is not law under Art. 141). [09]
  - Secondment: CBIC Instruction 05/2023-GST (13.12.2023, per [04]; not read) tells officers not to invoke the extended period without suppression.
- **Result:** quashed (SC); or demand re-worked under s.73 if within its time limit (s.75(2)).
- **Fix:** add a separate SCN paragraph: *"Grounds for invoking section 74(1): (a) the noticee was required to declare ___ in ___ but did not (Explanation 2); (b) evidence: ___ (statement dated ___, documents at RUD ___); (c) intent to evade is shown by ___."* Pick the one ground that applies. Never write "fraud or wilful misstatement or suppression" as a list. If you cannot fill (a) to (c), issue under s.73 (or s.74A(5)(i)).

**B5. Documents relied on not supplied; third-party or inspection reports not disclosed**
- **Defect:** the SCN or order relies on a DGGI or State intimation, an investigation report, a supplier's statement or an inspection report that the noticee never saw.
- **Cases:** *S.S. Metals*, Madras HC: fake-invoice conclusion with no documentary basis set aside (remand on 20% pre-deposit). *Safecon* (above). *Umesh v. UoI*, Delhi HC, 11.09.2024 (inspection based on unnamed neighbours); *F R Trade Links*, Kerala HC, 05.08.2021 (officer must hold own enquiry, not forward an intelligence report); Delhi HC (DB) Sept 2024 (no verification date or material); *Om Impex*, Bombay HC, Oct 2024 (documents relied on not supplied). Persuasive. [02], [06], [09]
- **Result:** remanded or quashed.
- **Fix:** annex a "List of Relied Upon Documents (RUDs)" to every SCN and serve them (or state a date and place for inspection). Summarise any third-party report in the SCN and attach it, redacting only what must be kept confidential. For inspections, record date, time, officer, persons met, photographs and GPS.

**B6. Interest or penalty not proposed or quantified in the SCN**
- **Defect:** the SCN demands tax only; the order adds interest or a penalty, or names no penalty clause.
- **Law:** s.73(1)/s.74(1)/s.74A(1) require the SCN to ask why tax "along with interest ... and a penalty" should not be paid. s.75(7): the order cannot exceed the amount in the notice. s.75(9) (interest payable whether or not specified in the **order**) does not save an SCN that never proposed it.
- **Cases:** *Sanjay Construction v. State of U.P.*, Allahabad HC, Feb 2026: unquantified interest quashed. Persuasive. [03]
- **Fix:** in the SCN's demand paragraph, list for each issue: tax (CGST/SGST/IGST/cess), interest under s.50(1) or s.50(3) "at the applicable rate from ___ till payment", and penalty under the exact clause with the amount. Quantify the interest up to the SCN date where you can.

**B7. One SCN covering several financial years**
- **Defect:** a single SCN or order for several years.
- **Cases (courts are split):**
  - **Telangana (binding):** *Goldstone Infra v. Addl. Commr., Ranga Reddy CGST Commissionerate*, Telangana HC (DB), 27.01.2026, W.P. 1498/2026: a composite s.74 SCN and order for several years upheld; no prejudice if each year is decided within its own s.74(10) limit. **BINDING IN TELANGANA.** [03]
  - **Against:** *Titan Company*, Madras HC (single judge, 18.12.2023; affirmed by a DB 21.03.2025), WP 33164/2023 (s.73(10) runs year by year; directed a year-wise split); *Milroc Good Earth*, Bombay HC (Goa) and *Rithwik Projects*, Bombay HC (DB) June 2026 (demand quashed); AP HC 2026 (composite orders set aside, fresh year-wise proceedings allowed); Kerala HC (reported). Persuasive. *Bangalore Golf Club* (Karnataka single judge) was **reversed by a Karnataka DB on 23.04.2026** (W.A. 1751/2024 & batch); do not cite it. *RR Metal Industries v. UoI*, Telangana HC: an earlier interim stay of a composite ss.74/74A/130 SCN (interim only). [03], [05]
  - **For:** *Ambika Traders*, Delhi HC 2025 (consolidation permissible and in fraud cases "necessary"; SLP dismissed as not pressed, so no SC law); *Technosys*, *Mathur Polymers* (Delhi); Karnataka DB, 23.04.2026. Persuasive. [03]
  - Bombay HC (DB) in *Rollmet LLP*, 17.04.2026, referred the question to a Larger Bench; outcome not found. [09]
- **Telangana: a composite multi-year SCN is lawful** (*Goldstone Infra*).
- **Safer practice (recommended, not a legal requirement):** one SCN and one order per financial year, each within that year's own limitation; it keeps limitation separate for each year and the orders cleaner. If one investigation covers several years, issue separate SCNs on the same date, or at minimum keep year-wise facts, computations, limitation dates and findings. Do not combine s.74, s.74A and s.130 in one notice.

**B8. Duplicate SCNs or orders for the same period**
- **Defect:** two notices or orders for the same period and issue (CGST and State, or the same office twice).
- **Cases:** Telangana HC, W.P. 20731/2025 batch, common order 15.10.2025: duplicates are errors rectifiable under s.161; the State accepted that the six-month bar does not apply (second proviso). **BINDING IN TELANGANA.** *Ennem Excel Engineering*, Telangana HC, 08.01.2026: directed rectification. **BINDING IN TELANGANA.** A May 2026 CGST case applied s.161 to duplicate orders. [03], [06]
- **Result:** directions to rectify.
- **Fix:** before issue, check the portal for any SCN or order for that period. If a duplicate exists, withdraw or rectify by a speaking s.161 order, with a DRC-08 summary (rule 142(7)).

**B9. No SCN at all: interest or tax recovered straight through DRC-07 or s.79**
- **Defect:** interest or tax demanded by a DRC-07 or a recovery letter without adjudication.
- **Cases:** *Mahadeo Construction*, Jharkhand HC 2020; *East India Udyog*, Jharkhand HC, 13.03.2024: disputed interest needs an SCN under s.73/74. *Reliance Formulation*, Gujarat HC, 27.06.2025, and *Bombay Art*, Gujarat HC, 10.10.2025: s.50(1) interest on self-assessed tax is recoverable under s.79 only after DRC-01D (rule 142B); a DRC-13 attachment was quashed. Persuasive. [08]
- **Fix:** undisputed self-assessed tax or interest: issue DRC-01D (rule 142B(1), 7 days to pay), then recover. Disputed amount: issue an SCN. A DRC-07 is only the summary of an order; it is never the order.

### Stage C. Service and limitation

**C1. Service only on the portal, in a tab the taxpayer did not see**
- **Defect:** SCN, hearing notices or order placed only under the portal's "Additional Notices & Orders" tab; ex parte order follows.
- **Law:** s.169(1) lists the modes: (a) by hand or courier, (b) registered or speed post with acknowledgement, (c) e-mail to the registered address, (d) the common portal, and then newspaper or affixing. Any one is legally valid. s.160(2): service cannot be questioned if the person acted on the notice.
- **Cases:**
  - *ACE Cardiopathy Solutions v. UoI*, Delhi HC (DB), 2024:DHC:4108-DB, and many later cases (*Neelgiri Machinery* 2025; W.P.(C) 10696/2026): ex parte orders set aside. Persuasive. [03]
  - Telangana HC (*Bollant Industries*, *Sri Sai Ram Traders*, *Win Creatives*): merits not examined; appeal allowed with condonation to be considered sympathetically. **BINDING IN TELANGANA.** [05]
- **Split on remedy:** Delhi quashes; Telangana relegates. Both treat portal-only service as weak.
- **Result:** quashed (Delhi) or relegated to appeal (Telangana).
- **Fix:** serve every SCN, hearing notice and order on the portal **and** by e-mail to the registered e-mail, and by speed post/RPAD where the taxpayer has not responded. In the order, add: *"The SCN was served on ___ through the common portal (RFN ___), by e-mail to ___ on ___ and by speed post (article no. ___) on ___. Hearing notices dated ___ were served in the same manner."* Portal documents with an RFN need no DIN (Circular 249/06/2025-GST, read in full); other communications need a DIN (Circulars 122/41/2019 and 128/47/2019).

**C2. SCN issued too late: s.73(2) or s.74(2)**
- **Defect:** SCN issued less than 3 months (s.73) or 6 months (s.74) before the deadline for the order.
- **Law:** s.73(2): SCN "at least three months prior to the time limit specified in sub-section (10)". s.74(2): "at least six months prior". s.73(10): order within 3 years from the due date of the annual return for that year (or the date of erroneous refund); s.74(10): 5 years.
- **Cases:** *Balaji Filling Station v. STO*, Telangana HC: SCN dated 28.12.2023 for FY 2017-18 set aside (on the State's concession, so limited weight). **BINDING IN TELANGANA.** AP HC (DB), 2025 (party not found): s.73(2) is mandatory; an SCN for FY 2020-21 issued 30.11.2024 (deadline 28.11.2024) was two days late and could not be condoned. Persuasive. [03], [05]
- **Result:** quashed.
- **Fix:** keep a limitation sheet in every file: due date of annual return → s.73(10)/74(10) order deadline (after any lawful extension) → latest SCN date (minus 3 or 6 months, counted exactly). Write the computation into the SCN: *"Due date of annual return for FY ___: ___. Time limit under s.73(10): ___. This notice is issued on ___, more than three months before that date."*

**C3. Order passed after the time limit; s.74A time limits**
- **Defect:** order issued after s.73(10)/74(10), or a s.74A order after 12 months from the SCN.
- **Law (statutory, no case found):**
  - s.75(10): proceedings are **deemed concluded** if the order is not issued within s.73(10), s.74(10) or s.74A(7).
  - s.74A applies from FY 2024-25 (s.74A(12)). SCN within 42 months of the annual-return due date or erroneous refund (s.74A(2)). Order within 12 months of the SCN (s.74A(7)), extendable by up to 6 months by the Commissioner or an authorised officer senior to the proper officer and not below Joint Commissioner, for reasons recorded in writing **before** the 12 months expire (proviso to s.74A(7)).
  - No s.74A SCN where the tax for a financial year is under Rs 1,000 (s.74A(1) proviso). (The 57th Council's Rs 10,000 recommendation is **not law**.)
  - s.75(1): any court or Tribunal stay is excluded. s.75(11): time spent in a departmental appeal on the same issue in other proceedings is excluded. s.75(3): an order pursuant to an appellate or court direction must be issued within 2 years of communication of the direction.
- **Fix:** for s.74A cases, diarise "SCN date + 12 months". If you need more time, put up a file note to the Commissioner/JC with the reasons for delay at least a month before expiry, and record the extension order's date in the final order.

**C4. Orders depending on the limitation-extension notifications (13/2022, 9/2023, 56/2023)**
- **Defect:** order for FY 2017-18 to 2019-20 passed within the extended dates, challenged on the ground that the notifications are invalid.
- **Cases:**
  - Not interfered with, but **not upheld**: *Brunda Infra v. Addl. Commr. of Central Tax*, W.P. 1154/2024 & batch (incl. *HCC-SEW-MEIL-AAG JV*), Telangana HC (DB), 02.01.2025. **BINDING IN TELANGANA.** The HC observed that a GST Council recommendation is a sine qua non and that later ratification cannot cure its absence (paras 94-97, on 56/2023), but decided the cases on the Supreme Court's Covid exclusion (15.03.2020 to 28.02.2022), so the validity of the notifications became academic (para 116). Allahabad HC upheld 9/2023 (*Graziano*); Patna HC upheld 56/2023 (name not confirmed). [03], [05]
  - Quashed: *Barkataki Print*, Gauhati HC, 19.09.2024 (56/2023 ultra vires: no prior Council recommendation); Madras HC, 12.06.2025 (9/2023 and 56/2023 illegal). Persuasive. [03]
  - **SC:** SLP (C) 4240/2025 (*HCC-SEW-MEIL-AAG JV*, from Telangana): notice 21.02.2025; **no judgment found by 14 Oct 2026.** Delhi HC remands such cases pending the SC. [09]
- **Courts split; SC pending.** The Telangana ruling binds you on the Covid exclusion; on the notifications themselves its observations are adverse. Never describe 9/2023 or 56/2023 as upheld by the Telangana HC.
- **Fix:** where possible, act within the un-extended period. Otherwise add: *"The time limit for this order stands extended by Notification No. ___-CT dated ___ issued under s.168A, with which the Hon'ble High Court of Telangana declined to interfere in Brunda Infra Pvt. Ltd. (W.P. No. 1154 of 2024 & batch, 02.01.2025). The validity of the notification is pending before the Hon'ble Supreme Court in SLP (C) No. 4240/2025; this order is subject to its outcome."* Also record the Covid exclusion under Notification 13/2022-CT where relied on.

### Stage D. Reply, hearing and evidence

**D1. No personal hearing, or a "hearing" in name only**
- **Defect:** order passed without offering a hearing because the taxpayer did not ask for one or ticked "no"; the hearing column shows "N.A."; or the notice gave no date, time or venue.
- **Law:** s.75(4): a hearing "shall be granted where a request is received in writing ... **or** where any adverse decision is contemplated". s.29(2) proviso (registration), s.30(2) proviso (revocation), s.107(8) (appeal), s.126(3) (penalty), s.129(4) (detention) each require a hearing.
- **Cases:**
  - *B.K. Steels v. Deputy State Tax Officer*, Telangana HC (DB), 25.07.2024, WP 18654 and 18766/2024: order set aside for no hearing; restored. **BINDING IN TELANGANA**, but passed **by consent** on an admitted breach, so its precedent weight is limited; the text of s.75(4) is the primary authority. [03]
  - *Rythu Seva Kendram v. Superintendent of Central Tax*, Telangana HC, 30.03.2026: appellate order without hearing set aside (a CGST matter). **BINDING IN TELANGANA.** [02]
  - *Sheeshmahal Enterprises*, Telangana HC (c. 2023): suo motu cancellation and the appellate order upholding it quashed for no hearing. **BINDING IN TELANGANA.** [06]
  - *Suresh Trading Corporation*, Madras HC (REG-17 gave no date and time); *Gateway Exim*, Gujarat HC (date, time and venue needed); Calcutta and Allahabad HCs ("N.A." is not a hearing); MP HC (DB) (hearing needed whether or not a reply was filed); Madras HC (reply and hearing are cumulative); Uttarakhand HC, Sept 2026 (*Shivtara*): not opting for a hearing does not dispense with it. Persuasive. [03], [06]
- **Result:** remanded for a hearing and fresh order (most common), or quashed.
- **Fix:** issue a separate, signed hearing notice with date, time, venue (or video link) and the officer's name, served by portal and e-mail. If the taxpayer does not attend, issue at least one more notice before deciding ex parte. In the order, add a "Personal hearing" paragraph: dates offered, mode of service, who attended, submissions made, or "did not appear despite notices dated ___ and ___".

**D2. Reply, documents or binding circulars ignored**
- **Defect:** the order does not deal with the reply, the documents produced, or a circular that binds the officer.
- **Cases:**
  - *DSV Air and Sea*, Telangana HC: refund rejection set aside for not considering detailed replies (consent remand). **BINDING IN TELANGANA** (limited weight). [01]
  - *Aavanti Solar Energy*, Karnataka HC: order on liquidated damages that never referred to Circular 178/10/2022 set aside and remitted. Persuasive. [07]
  - *GMA Pinnacle Automotives* (Kerala), *AP Studio Enterprises* (AP), *Contemporary Leather* (Madras): orders ignoring Circular 183 certificates set aside. Persuasive; case details not individually confirmed. [02]
  - *Rambabu*, Allahabad HC, Aug 2026: penalty remanded for ignoring the e-way bill, e-invoice and bilty produced. *Shri Shyam Footwear*, Delhi HC, 31.01.2023: a clerical error corrected in the reply must be considered. Persuasive. [08], [01]
- **Result:** remanded.
- **Fix:** structure the order as a table or numbered paragraphs: "Submission (para __ of reply) → Finding → Reason". List every document produced and say what it proves or why it fails. Name and apply every circular raised.

**D3. Adjournments**
- **Law:** s.75(5): adjourn for sufficient cause, with reasons in writing; no more than three adjournments in the proceedings. No case quashing an order for refusing a fourth adjournment was found in the files.
- **Fix:** record each request and your reasons for granting or refusing. After three, record that the statutory limit is reached and fix a final date.

**D4. New material used in the order without notice**
- **Defect:** the order relies on a report, statement or figure obtained after the SCN, not put to the noticee.
- **Cases:** *Om Impex* (Bombay), *Umesh* (Delhi), *F R Trade Links* (Kerala) [06]; *Lupin*, Bombay HC, 21.11.2025: objection raised for the first time in court; remanded [01].
- **Fix:** send any new material to the noticee with a short time to respond before the order. If it changes the ground or the amount, issue a supplementary SCN (see E1).

### Stage E. The order

**E1. Order goes beyond the SCN (s.75(7))**
- **Defect:** the order demands more than the SCN, or confirms on a ground, section or clause the SCN did not raise.
- **Law:** s.75(7): the amount in the order "shall not be in excess of the amount specified in the notice and no demand shall be confirmed on the grounds other than the grounds specified in the notice".
- **Cases:**
  - *Vrinda Automation v. State of U.P.*, Allahabad HC, May 2025 (Rs 1.35 crore against a Rs 66 lakh SCN); Karnataka HC, WP 18888/2025, Nov 2025 (Rs 6.93 crore against Rs 2.49 crore, and new grounds). Persuasive. [03]
  - *JK Technology v. Deputy STO*, Telangana HC: SCN under s.29(2)(e), cancellation under s.29(2)(c), retrospective; both quashed, registration restored. **BINDING IN TELANGANA.** [05], [06]
  - *A1 Adil Traders*, Telangana HC, 04.03.2025, W.P. 4539/2025: reasons added only in the order cannot save a bare SCN. **BINDING IN TELANGANA.** [06]
  - *Augustan Textile Colours*, GSTAT Ernakulam, Sep 2026: appellate finding on a ground not in the SCN vitiated the order. **GSTAT.** [09]
- **Result:** quashed (at least the excess); sometimes the whole order.
- **Fix:** before signing, put the SCN demand table next to the order demand table, issue by issue. Each confirmed figure must be equal or lower, and each ground must be one stated in the SCN. If investigation of the reply reveals a new ground or a larger amount, issue a supplementary SCN within the time limit (s.73(3)/74(3)/74A(3) statements are allowed only on the **same** grounds for other periods).

**E2. Non-speaking order: no reasons**
- **Defect:** the order recites the SCN and says "the reply is not acceptable", or gives conclusions without reasons.
- **Law:** s.75(6): the order "shall set out the relevant facts and the basis of his decision". s.107(12) (appellate order: points for determination, decision and reasons). Rule 92(3) (refund: reasons recorded in writing). Rule 23(2)(a) (revocation: reasons recorded).
- **Cases:**
  - Telangana HC, Nov 2025 (party not found): non-speaking appellate order on "intermediary" sent back. *Virtusa Systems*, Telangana HC: department failed to show the services were intermediary. **BINDING IN TELANGANA.** [01]
  - Gauhati HC 2026: REG-19 without reasons quashed. *Deccan Fine Chemicals*, Gujarat HC: partial refund rejection without reasons quashed. *Maharaj Ji Enterprises*, Patna HC, July 2025: reasons given only in the counter-affidavit do not count. Calcutta HC, 27.11.2025: cryptic rejection of a rectification application quashed. Persuasive. [06], [01]
- **Result:** remanded or quashed.
- **Fix:** for each issue write: facts found → evidence → the taxpayer's case → why it is accepted or rejected → the provision → the figure. Reasons must be in the order; nothing can be added later in court.

**E3. Order without findings on the s.74 ingredients; conversion to s.73**
- **Defect:** s.74 confirmed without a finding of fraud, wilful misstatement or suppression with intent; or s.74 fails and nobody re-determines under s.73.
- **Law:** s.75(2): where an appellate authority, Tribunal or court holds that fraud or suppression is not established, **the proper officer** determines the tax as if under s.73. s.75(2A): for s.74A, the penalty becomes the s.74A(5)(i) penalty.
- **Cases:**
  - *Raghuvansh Agro Farms*, Allahabad HC 2025: s.74 demand quashed for want of foundational findings. Madras HC (party not found): s.74 order remanded for re-decision under s.73. Persuasive. [08]
  - *Sterling & Wilson*, GSTAT PB, 11.02.2026: neither the first appellate authority nor GSTAT can itself confirm a s.73 demand; the proper officer must re-determine. **GSTAT.** [09]
  - *R B Pandey and Sons*, Gujarat HC, Feb 2026, SCA 8054/2025: no conversion where clear findings of fraud stood. Persuasive. [03]
  - Madras HC, 13.07.2026: where the order was wrongly passed under s.74, denial of s.128A waiver on that ground falls too. Persuasive. [08]
- **Whether s.73 time limit bars re-determination after s.74 fails is unsettled** [03].
- **Fix:** write a separate "Findings on invocation of section 74" with one sub-finding for each ingredient and the evidence. If the evidence is not there, confirm only under s.73 and only if the s.73(10) time limit is still open. Circular 254/11/2025-GST (assigning the proper officer under s.74A) should be read for s.75(2) cases; it was not read for this file.

**E4. Order or DRC-07 without a DIN/RFN, or unsigned** — see B2. Same cases, same fix.

**E5. Binding circular or binding ruling not followed**
- **Defect:** the order ignores a circular that binds the officer or a Telangana HC/SC ruling.
- **Cases:** *Kamlakshi Finance*, *Ratan Melting* (SC, above A5). *Micro Systems and Services*, Telangana HC, 05.09.2022: Circular 173/05/2022 is clarificatory and applies from 31.03.2020; refund remanded. **BINDING IN TELANGANA.** *Aavanti Solar* (Circular 178), Circular 183 cases (above D2). [01], [04], [07]
- **Fix:** where a circular or binding ruling applies, apply it and cite it. If you think it is wrong, follow it anyway and note that the department may appeal. Where a ruling you rely on is under SC challenge, record the pendency.

**E6. Estimation instead of the prescribed method**
- **Defect:** amount worked out by estimate where a rule prescribes a formula.
- **Cases:** *Hemraj Rice Mill v. AC, CGST & CX*, Calcutta HC, 16.06.2025, WPA 3159/2025 (a CGST case): rule 42/43 reversal estimated; remanded. A 2026 ruling (court not identified) quashed a rule 42 reversal where GSTR-9 Table 5 figures were non-GST, not exempt supplies. Persuasive. [02]
- **Fix:** annex the rule 42/43 computation (or rule 88B interest, or rule 89 refund) with each input figure and its source. Confirm that the turnover used is actually "exempt supply".

**E7. Penalty: wrong provision, double penalty, or no penalty reasons**
- **Law (all verified in the Act):**
  - s.75(13): where a penalty is imposed under s.73, s.74 or s.74A, no penalty for the same act under any other provision.
  - s.125: only where no penalty is separately provided; up to Rs 25,000.
  - s.126(1): no penalty for minor breaches (tax under Rs 5,000) or easily rectifiable documentary mistakes made without fraudulent intent or gross negligence. s.126(3): hearing. s.126(4): the order must specify the nature of the breach and the law under which the amount is fixed. s.126(6): s.126 does not apply where the penalty is a fixed sum or fixed percentage.
  - s.73(8): no penalty if tax and interest are paid within 30 days of the SCN; but s.73(11): penalty still payable where self-assessed tax or tax collected was not paid within 30 days of the due date. s.74A(8)(ii) and (11): same, with 60 days.
  - s.74 Explanation 1(ii) and s.74A Explanation 1(ii): where proceedings against the main person are concluded, proceedings against co-noticees for s.122/s.125 penalty are deemed concluded.
- **Cases:** *Kandan Hardware Mart* and *NKR Traders*, Madras HC 2026: no s.125 penalty on top of a s.47 late fee. *Global Plastowares*, Kerala HC 2023: s.73(8) does not help where tax was collected and not deposited; s.73(11) applies. Persuasive. [08]
- **Fix:** for each penalty write the clause, the act or omission, and why the amount follows. Check s.75(13), s.125, s.126 and s.73(8)/(11) before confirming.

**E8. Rectification under s.161 misused, or rectification refused without reasons**
- **Law:** s.161: rectify an error apparent on the face of the record, on own motion or when pointed out within 3 months of the order; not after 6 months, except purely clerical or arithmetical errors; where rectification adversely affects any person, natural justice must be followed (third proviso). DRC-08 summary (rule 142(7)).
- **Cases:** *Sajal Kumar Das*, Calcutta HC 2024 (substantive rewriting quashed); *Future Consumer Ltd v. Union of India*, Delhi HC, 10.10.2025, W.P.(C) 15611/2025 (a personal hearing is mandatory under the third proviso to s.161; there, a rectification application was rejected without one); Calcutta HC, 27.11.2025 (cryptic rejection quashed); Madras HC, Dec 2025 (s.161 cannot create a fresh demand). Persuasive. Telangana HC W.P. 20731/2025 batch (duplicates rectifiable). **BINDING IN TELANGANA.** Telangana HC 2026 directed s.161 applications to be decided within three weeks after a hearing. [06]
- **Fix:** use s.161 only for errors visible on the record (wrong figure, duplicate demand, overlooked payment). Anything needing argument or new evidence goes to a fresh SCN or appeal. Before any rectification that increases liability or reduces a refund, issue a notice and hear the taxpayer. Pass a speaking order; reject an application only with reasons, and after a hearing (*Future Consumer*).

### Stage F. Recovery and attachment

**F1. Recovery within 3 months of the order without recorded reasons (s.78)**
- **Defect:** DRC-13 garnishee notice, bank letter or ledger debit before 3 months from service of the order.
- **Law:** s.78: pay within 3 months of service; proviso: the proper officer may require earlier payment "for reasons to be recorded in writing". **Instruction 01/2024-GST, 30.05.2024 (read in full):** under Circular 3/3/2017-GST the proper officer for the s.78 proviso is the **jurisdictional Principal Commissioner/Commissioner**. The Deputy/Assistant Commissioner must put up the case with reasons; the Commissioner records specific reasons (e.g. high risk to revenue, likely closure of business, declining finances, impending insolvency), based on credible evidence, and issues directions. Directions must not be issued mechanically. Only if the taxpayer then fails to pay does the DC/AC recover under s.79.
- **Cases:** *Cargotec India*, Madras HC, May 2024: early recovery set aside and amount re-credited. A 2026 HC (court not named): same-day ledger debit violated s.78. *Liakhat Ali Mallick*, Calcutta HC 2023: where reasons were recorded, the garnishee order stood (stayed on conditions because of appeal). Persuasive. [03]
- **Result:** recovery set aside, amount refunded or re-credited.
- **Fix:** no recovery step before 3 months unless the Commissioner's written direction under the s.78 proviso is on file and served. Quote its date in the DRC-13.

**F2. Recovery continued after appeal and pre-deposit**
- **Law:** s.107(7): once the s.107(6) pre-deposit is paid, recovery of the balance is deemed stayed. s.112(9): same for the Tribunal after the s.112(8) deposit. **Current pre-deposit (Act text):** first appeal 10% of disputed tax, capped at Rs 20 crore (s.107(6)(b)); penalty-only orders 10% of the penalty (s.107(6) proviso, w.e.f. 01.10.2025). Tribunal: a further 10% of disputed tax, capped at Rs 20 crore (s.112(8)(b), as substituted by s.143 Finance (No.2) Act 2024), and 10% of penalty for penalty-only orders (s.112(8) proviso, w.e.f. 01.10.2025).
- **Circular 224/18/2024-GST (11.07.2024, read in full):** where the first appeal is decided and the Tribunal appeal could not be filed, the taxpayer may pay an amount equal to the s.112(8) pre-deposit against the demand in the Electronic Liability Register and file an undertaking that he will appeal to the Tribunal; recovery of the balance then stands stayed under s.112(9).
- **Cases:** Calcutta and Delhi HCs: attachments to be lifted once the appeal is filed with pre-deposit. Telangana HC, Apr 2026 (party not found): directed compliance with Circular 224 within 15 days. **BINDING IN TELANGANA.** That report speaks of "20%"; the current s.112(8) figure is 10% (see Gaps). *PVCON Engineering*, SC, 07.09.2026 (interim): a GSTAT appeal to be heard without pre-deposit for a penalty-only order, pending the SLP. *Manoranjan Dash*, GSTAT Cuttack 2026: credit for the first-stage deposit. [03], [05], [09]
- **Fix:** before any recovery step, check the portal for an appeal (APL-01/APL-05) and for payments mapped to the demand. If the pre-deposit and appeal (or the Circular 224 undertaking) exist, stop and lift any attachment. Do not take coercive steps against an appellant who holds an SC interim order.

**F3. Provisional attachment kept beyond one year (s.83(2))**
- **Law:** s.83(2): an attachment "shall cease to have effect after the expiry of a period of one year from the date of the order". Rule 159(2): the encumbrance is removed on the Commissioner's instructions or on expiry of one year, whichever is earlier.
- **Cases:** *Kesari Nandan Mobile*, **SC**, 2025 INSC 983: lapses automatically after one year; pending proceedings do not keep it alive. *Greenfinch Team Management* (22.12.2022, WP 44235/2022), *KMC Constructions v. Pr. Commr. of Central Tax* (a CGST case) and *Aarush Enterprises* (2024), Telangana HC. **BINDING IN TELANGANA.** *Krish Overseas*, Delhi HC (DB), 2024. [03], [05]
- **Result:** quashed; bank told to allow operation.
- **Fix:** diarise "order date + 1 year" and write to the bank before that date releasing the account. A fresh attachment needs a fresh order on fresh material.

**F4. Provisional attachment without the Commissioner's opinion on tangible material**
- **Law:** s.83(1): only after initiation of proceedings under Chapter XII, XIV or XV; the Commissioner must form the opinion that attachment is necessary to protect revenue; order in writing (DRC-22, rule 159(1)). Rule 159(5): objection in DRC-22A; the Commissioner may release after a hearing.
- **Cases:** *Radha Krishan Industries v. State of H.P.*, **SC**, 20.04.2021, CA 1155/2021: opinion must rest on tangible material; strict compliance; not to be used to harass; a Joint Commissioner acts as the Commissioner's delegate. [03]
- **Fix:** the file note must state the proceeding already initiated (with date), the material showing risk to revenue (e.g. diversion of funds, closure), and why attachment is necessary and proportionate. Decide any DRC-22A objection after a hearing, by a speaking order.

**F5. Wrong account or wrong mode under s.79**
- **Cases:** Madras HC, 03.04.2026 (party not found): an overdraft account holds the bank's money and cannot be attached under s.79. Persuasive. [03]
- **Fix:** in a garnishee notice, target current/savings credit balances and receivables, not OD/CC limits.

**F6. Rule 86A blocking of the credit ledger**
- **Law:** rule 86A(1): Commissioner or an officer not below Assistant Commissioner, with "reasons to believe" on the specified grounds, "for reasons to be recorded in writing". Rule 86A(3): the restriction ceases after one year.
- **Cases:** *Laxmi Fine Chem v. AC*, Telangana HC, 2024, WP 5256/2024: no negative blocking; only the balance available may be blocked. **BINDING IN TELANGANA.** *Bhavani Oxides v. State of Telangana*, Telangana HC, 26.06.2024: block without adequate reasons and natural justice cannot survive. **BINDING IN TELANGANA.** *Samay Alloys* (Gujarat), *Best Crop Science* (Delhi), *New Nalbandh Traders* (Gujarat: no borrowed satisfaction; post-decisional hearing in two weeks), *Shri Sai Ram Enterprises* (Delhi, 28.04.2025: block over one year lifted). [02], [09]
- **Fix:** record your own reasons on the material (not "as per DGGI letter"); block only the balance available on that date and record it; offer a hearing promptly; diarise the one-year lapse. For ITC already utilised, issue an SCN under s.73/74/74A. (The 57th Council's recommended right to object is not yet law.)

**F7. Interest recovered without DRC-01D or SCN** — see B9.

**F8. Refund silently adjusted against dues; sanctioned refund recovered without review**
- **Cases:** Delhi HC, W.P.(C) 3967/2025, Aug 2025 (party unclear): an accepted cash-ledger refund adjusted against dues was treated as payable. Persuasive. *Gupta Battery House v. State of Telangana*, Telangana HC 2026 (**interim only**): questioned recovering a sanctioned refund under s.73 without first reviewing or appealing the sanction order; appellate order stayed. [09]
- **Fix:** adjust only under s.54(10) or s.79 with notice and an order. Before recovering an erroneous refund under s.73/74, record why the sanction order was not reviewed or appealed under s.107(2), or do that first.

**F9. Recovery from a cancelled registrant (department's defence)**
- *Manikanta Electronics Services Center v. State of Telangana*, Telangana HC (DB), WP 1362/2026: cancellation does not affect liability for earlier periods (s.29(3)); taxpayer left to appeal. **BINDING IN TELANGANA.** [06] Serve such persons by e-mail and post as well as the portal.

### Stage G. Appeal stage

This covers orders you pass as appellate authority (where assigned) and points the department must watch when its orders reach appeal.

**G1. Condonation of delay rejected without hearing**
- **Law:** s.107(8): the Appellate Authority "shall give an opportunity to the appellant of being heard".
- **Cases:** Telangana HC (party not found): dismissal for delay without hearing the condonation application set aside; in a 2026 case the State conceded it was "in the teeth of" s.107(8). **BINDING IN TELANGANA.** [03], [05]
- **Fix:** issue a hearing notice on the condonation application and pass a reasoned order on "sufficient cause".

**G2. Condoning delay beyond the statutory limit (and the opposite)**
- **Law:** s.107(1): 3 months from communication (6 months for a departmental application under s.107(2)); s.107(4): a further one month only.
- **Cases:** *Singh Enterprises v. CCE*, **SC** (2008, citation not confirmed) and *AC (CT) LTU, Kakinada v. GlaxoSmithKline Consumer Health Care*, **SC**, 06.05.2020, (2020) 19 SCC 681 (an AP VAT case): a statutory authority cannot go beyond the statutory period, and a writ cannot be used to bypass it. The SC stayed (30.08.2024) an HC ruling applying s.5 of the Limitation Act to s.107, and issued notice (July/Aug 2026) on SLPs about appeals beyond the s.107(4) cap. **Pending.** [03]
- **But:** where the Telangana HC itself grants a window (2 weeks to 45 days) to appeal with condonation and pre-deposit, the appellate authority must entertain the appeal and decide it on merits (Telangana HC Jan 2025 batch; a later rejection as time-barred was set aside). **BINDING IN TELANGANA.** [05]
- **Safe course:** do not condone beyond 3 + 1 months on your own; comply fully with any HC direction.

**G3. Appellate order without hearing, or non-speaking** — see D1 (*Rythu Seva Kendram*, *Sheeshmahal*) and E2 (Telangana HC non-speaking intermediary order). s.107(12) requires points for determination, decision and reasons.

**G4. Appellate authority remanding, or itself confirming a s.73 demand after s.74 fails**
- **Law:** s.107(11): the Appellate Authority confirms, modifies or annuls but "shall not refer the case back". Second proviso: no order requiring payment of tax not demanded unless a show cause notice is given and the order is within the s.73/74/74A time limit.
- **Cases:** Telangana HC, Nov 2025: appellate authority cannot remand and must decide (**BINDING IN TELANGANA**) [01]. *Sterling & Wilson*, GSTAT PB: only the proper officer can re-determine under s.73 after s.74 fails (commentators note the tension with s.107(11)) [09].
- **Fix:** decide the appeal yourself. Where s.74 fails, set aside the s.74 finding and record that the proper officer may re-determine under s.75(2) within the s.73 time limit.

**G5. Appellate finding on a ground not in the SCN** — *Augustan Textile Colours*, GSTAT Ernakulam (above E1). Keep the appellate decision within the SCN's grounds.

**G6. Refund refused because the department "proposes to appeal"**
- **Law:** s.107(16): an appellate order is final and binding subject to further appeal. s.54(11): the Commissioner may withhold a refund only where the order is the subject of an appeal or other proceeding, he is of the opinion that the refund is likely to adversely affect revenue "on account of malfeasance or fraud", and after giving a hearing.
- **Cases:** *ADV Industrial Services*, Telangana HC (DB), Sep 2026: RFD-06 rejecting a Rs 19.33 crore consequential refund because the department planned to go to GSTAT quashed. **BINDING IN TELANGANA.** Orissa HC (W.P.(C) 17470/2026), Delhi HC (W.P.(C) 15557/2024), Patna HC (*KPIL-JWIL JV*, 13.08.2026) agree. [01], [09]
- **Fix:** process the consequential refund. If it must be held, file the appeal first, then obtain the Commissioner's reasoned s.54(11) order after a hearing.

### Stage H. Issue-specific grounds

**H1. ITC denied because the supplier did not pay tax (s.16(2)(c))**
- **Defect:** ITC reversed on an arithmetic 2A/3B gap or a supplier's default, without verifying non-payment or looking at the buyer's evidence.
- **Cases:**
  - *Bhandari Scrap Traders v. UoI*, **SC**, 24.07.2026, SLP(C) 23931/2026: affirmed *Maruti Enterprise* (Gujarat HC, 01.05.2026); s.16(2)(c) is valid and not to be read down for bona fide buyers; Delhi VAT cases (*Arise India*, *On Quest*, *Shanti Kiran*) do not carry over. Check whether it is a reasoned order. [02]
  - *Sahil Enterprises*, Tripura HC, 06.01.2026 (read-down): **stayed by the SC** (Diary 33460/2026). Do not follow. [09]
  - *Suncraft Energy*, Calcutta HC (SLP dismissed 14.12.2023) and *D.Y. Beathel*, Madras HC, 24.02.2021: proceed against the supplier first; recipient reversal as a last resort. Persuasive; substantively limited by *Bhandari*, but the procedural point is still cited. [02]
  - *Inam Impex v. AC (ST)*, Telangana HC, 31.03.2026: relegated to appeal; a summary notes the SCN rested on an arithmetic excess without a verified finding. **BINDING IN TELANGANA.** Telangana batch WP 6880 etc. of 2025 pending. [02]
- **Result now:** demand sustainable on law, but orders without a verified finding get remanded.
- **Fix:** in SCN and order: (a) verified finding that the supplier did not pay (supplier's GSTR-3B and cash/credit ledger, not just 2A/3B); (b) steps taken against the supplier and the result; (c) the buyer's invoices, payments and movement evidence considered; (d) note that ITC can be re-availed when the supplier pays. Use s.74 only where the buyer's own fraud or collusion is shown (*Safecon*).

**H2. ITC time limit (s.16(4)) and the 2024 relief (s.16(5), s.16(6))**
- **Law (Act text):** s.16(4): no ITC after 30 November following the end of the financial year or the annual return, whichever is earlier. s.16(5) (inserted by s.118 Finance (No.2) Act 2024): for FY 2017-18 to 2020-21, ITC may be taken in any return filed up to 30.11.2021. s.16(6): ITC after revocation of cancellation, where it was not time-barred on the date of cancellation. Circular 237/31/2024-GST (15.10.2024) gives a rectification route for orders under ss.73/74/107/108 that denied ITC now saved.
- **Cases:** s.16(4) valid: *Thirumalakonda Plywoods*, AP HC, 18.07.2023 (persuasive, strongly so as a sister State); *Gobinda Construction*, Patna HC, 08.09.2023 (SC notice Jan 2024; outcome not found). s.16(5) applied to set aside demands: *H.R. Constructions*, AP HC, 06.08.2026; Calcutta and Madras HCs (names not confirmed). *Venus Infra Projects*, Madras HC 2026: s.16(6) does not revive ITC already time-barred on the cancellation date. GSTAT (Sep 2026, bench not found): s.16(5) relief does not wipe out s.50(1) interest on tax paid late. [02], [05], [09]
- **Fix:** every s.16(4) SCN and order must contain a paragraph: *"Applicability of s.16(5)/(6): the invoice relates to FY ___; the return claiming the ITC was filed on ___; [s.16(5) therefore applies / does not apply because ___]."* Drop covered demands. Examine s.50(1) interest separately.

**H3. GSTR-2A/2B vs GSTR-3B mismatch**
- **Law:** s.16(2)(aa) (ITC only where the invoice is furnished by the supplier and communicated) applies from 01.01.2022 (inserted by s.109 Finance Act 2021). Circular 183/15/2022-GST (FY 2017-18 and 2018-19) and Circular 193/05/2023-GST (01.04.2019 to 31.12.2021): supplier or CA certificate procedure.
- **Cases:** *Diya Agencies v. STO*, Kerala HC, 12.09.2023 (pre-2022 denial on 2A alone set aside; recipient must prove genuineness); *Biocon*, Karnataka HC 2024 (no matching requirement before 01.01.2022; imports and SEZ). Circular 183 cases (above D2). Karnataka HC 2024: apply Circular 183 even if the assessee did not cite it. *Sterling & Wilson*, GSTAT: mismatch alone is no ground for s.74. Persuasive; GSTAT. [02], [06], [09]
- **Fix:** for pre-2022 periods, never rest the demand on 2A non-reflection alone; call for and examine the Circular 183/193 certificates and other evidence of genuineness, and discuss them in the order. Do not invoke s.74 for a mismatch without evidence of intent.

**H4. Fake invoices, non-existent or retrospectively cancelled suppliers**
- **Law:** s.155: the burden of proving eligibility for ITC is on the claimant.
- **Cases:**
  - *State of Karnataka v. Ecom Gill Coffee Trading*, **SC**, 13.03.2023, CA 230/2023 (KVAT): the dealer must prove the genuineness of the transaction and physical movement of goods; invoices and bank payment alone are not enough. Supports the department. [02]
  - Retrospective cancellation of the supplier is not enough by itself: *LGW Industries*, Calcutta HC, Dec 2021 (WPA 23512/2019); *Gargo Traders*, Calcutta HC, 12.06.2023; *Fathima Traders*, Madras HC. Persuasive. No Telangana merits ruling found. [02]
  - *Safecon Lifescience* (s.74 needs buyer-specific proof and disclosure); *S.S. Metals* (documents must be supplied). [09], [02]
  - Department wins where evidence is solid: *Roshan Sharma*, Calcutta HC, 24.07.2024 (supplier found non-existent); Delhi HC 2025 and MP HC Dec 2024 decline writs in fraud cases. [02]
- **Fix:** findings supplier by supplier: registration status on the invoice date; inspection result (with date); e-way bills, transport, freight, weighbridge, stock entries called for; what was produced and why it fails; payment trail and any round-tripping. Attach the investigation material. Do not rely only on the later cancellation date.

**H5. E-way bill detention and confiscation (s.129, s.130)**
- **Law (Act/Rules text):** s.129(1): no detention or seizure without serving an order of detention. s.129(3): notice within 7 days of detention, specifying the penalty; order within 7 days of service of the notice. s.129(4): no penalty without a hearing. s.130(1): confiscation for supply or receipt "with intent to evade" and the other listed grounds; s.130(2): option to pay fine in lieu. Rule 138C(1): Part A of EWB-03 within 24 hours and Part B within 3 days of inspection. s.126(1): no penalty for minor, easily rectifiable breaches without fraudulent intent.
- **Cases:**
  - *Satyam Shivam Papers v. AC (ST)*, Telangana HC, 02.06.2021, WP 9688/2020: e-way bill expired because a rally blocked roads; no material suggesting evasion; tax and penalty set aside **with costs on the officer**. SC dismissed the department's SLP on 12.01.2022 (costs reportedly enhanced; amount unverified). **BINDING IN TELANGANA.** [03], [05]
  - *Auto Industries*, Allahabad HC, Nov 2025 (missing Part-B; no penalty without intent); Allahabad 2025 (wrong dispatch place; vehicle number; breakdown); *Nancy Trading*, Allahabad HC, 15.07.2024 (missing e-invoice, other documents present). Kerala HC 2022: officer must consider the explanation. Persuasive. [03], [08]
  - s.130: *Panchhi Traders*, Gujarat HC (DB), 11.12.2025: ss.129 and 130 independent; s.130 can be invoked directly where facts show clear intent to evade, with the opinion formed within the rule 138B/138C timelines; otherwise release under s.129. Earlier *Moon Traders* (Gujarat 2020) required the sequence. Uttarakhand HC, June 2025: confiscation completed in a single day quashed. Persuasive. No Telangana s.130 ruling found. [08]
- **Courts split** on s.129/s.130 sequence. **Safe course:** use s.130 only on recorded material showing intent, formed within the rule 138C time frame, with a proper notice and hearing.
- **Fix:** in MOV-07 and MOV-09, record the explanation offered (and documents produced), then a finding: is there a real tax risk (no e-way bill, goods mismatch, under-invoicing) or only a technical lapse with a plausible explanation? A genuine, explained lapse should not be penalised. Keep to the 7 + 7 day timeline. (57th Council recommendations on interception and s.130 in transit are **not law**.)

**H6. Interest (s.50)**
- **Law (Act/Rules text):** s.50(1) proviso (substituted from 01.07.2017 by s.112 Finance Act 2021; in force 01.06.2021 by Notification 16/2021-CT): for a return filed late, interest only on the portion paid from the cash ledger, **except** where the return is filed after proceedings under s.73/74/74A have commenced. s.50(3) (substituted from 01.07.2017 by s.111 Finance Act 2022): interest on ITC "wrongly availed **and** utilised". Rule 88B(3): from the date of utilisation to the date of reversal or payment.
- **Cases:** *Utpal Das*, Calcutta HC, Oct 2024 (no interest without utilisation); *Manoja Kumar Nayak*, Orissa HC, Apr 2026 (utilisation tested on the total ledger balance, not head-wise; credit for pre-SCN reversal; penalty fell). Contra in part: Madras HC, June 2026 (party not found), reading s.50(3) widely to cover 2A/3B mismatches, but still tied to utilisation. *Megha Engineering*, Telangana HC 2019 (gross-liability interest): overtaken by the retrospective proviso; do not cite. *Sanjay Construction* (interest must be proposed in SCN). [05], [08], [03]
- **Fix:** s.50(1): compute on the cash portion only; if charging on the gross, record the date proceedings began and the return date. s.50(3): annex a month-wise table of the electronic credit ledger balance against the disputed ITC; charge interest only for the days the balance fell below the disputed amount; give credit for reversals.

**H7. Penalty (s.73/74/74A, s.122, s.122(1A), s.125)**
- **Law (Act text):** s.122(1A) (inserted by s.126 Finance Act 2020, in force 01.01.2021): "any person who retains the benefit of a transaction covered under clauses (i), (ii), (vii) or (ix) of sub-section (1) and at whose instance such transaction is conducted" is liable to a penalty equal to the tax evaded or ITC availed or passed on. Other penalty law: see E7.
- **Cases:**
  - *Patanjali Ayurved v. UoI*, Allahabad HC, 29.05.2025, Writ Tax 1603/2024: s.122 penalty is civil; mens rea needed for prosecution, not penalty; s.122 proceedings are independent of s.73/74. Persuasive. [08]
  - **s.122(1A), courts split:** Bombay HC (*Amit Manilal Haria*, 2025): only a taxable person; Revenue's SLP reportedly dismissed with the question kept open (single source). Delhi HC 2025 and Gauhati HC (*Mayank Bansal*, 2026): any person who retains the benefit. One HC (not identified): not for acts before 01.01.2021 (Art. 20(1)). No SC or Telangana ruling. [08]
- **Safe course for s.122(1A):** use it only for acts on or after 01.01.2021; record the person's role, the transaction clause of s.122(1) and the benefit retained, with evidence.

**H8. Classification, rate, valuation and scope of supply**
- **Cases (classification):** *Welkin Foods*, **SC**, Jan 2026 (apply the GRIs in sequence; GRI 1 and the notes first; single source); *Cadila Health Care*, **SC**, 05.10.2026 (classify by the form of goods at sale, not end use); *Madhan Agro*, **SC**, 18.12.2024 (packaging and Chapter 33 Note 3 decide, not possible use); *Sri Vijaya Visakha Milk*, AP HC, 10.12.2024 (flavoured milk 0402; SC dismissed Revenue's challenge, May 2025); *X'SS Beverage*, Gauhati HC, 04.03.2025; *STS-KEC JV*, Madras HC, 28.01.2025 (railway works under entry 3(v) at 12%; nature of work decides). *Sahasra Enterprises*, Telangana HC: rate disputes on facts go to appeal. **BINDING IN TELANGANA.** A rate dispute alone is not suppression (*G.R. Infra*, SC). [07], [03]
- **Cases (scope and value):** *Krazybee*, Karnataka HC (liquidated damages SCN quashed, Circular 178/10/2022); *Aavanti Solar* (order not discussing Circular 178 set aside). Leasehold assignment not a supply (*Gujarat Chamber*, *Aerocom Cushions*; SLPs dismissed 2026). *Prahitha Constructions*, Telangana HC, 09.02.2024 (TDR under JDA is taxable; SC notice, no stay) **BINDING IN TELANGANA** (supports the department). *Taghar Vasudeva Ambrish*, **SC**, 04.12.2025 (hostel leasing exempt up to 17.07.2022). *Care College of Nursing*, Telangana HC, 17.10.2023 and *Dilip Kumar*, **SC** (exemptions read strictly; burden on the claimant) — supports the department. Wrong head: *GR Tech Services*, Karnataka HC, June 2026 and Madras HC, 16.07.2026: adjust IGST paid before demanding CGST/SGST with interest and penalty. [07]
- **Fix:** classification paragraph in order: description and evidence (test report, catalogue, labels) → GRI 1 (heading text, section and chapter notes) → later GRIs only if needed → HSN Explanatory Notes → rate notification entry → why the taxpayer's heading fails. For damages, identify the separate promise to tolerate and apply Circular 178. For wrong-head cases, quantify the IGST paid and provide for adjustment (s.77(2): no interest on CGST/SGST where IGST was paid).

**H9. Registration: cancellation, suspension, retrospective date, revocation**
- **Law (Act/Rules text):** s.29(2): cancel "from such date, including any retrospective date, as he may deem fit", on clauses (a) to (e); proviso: not without a hearing. Rule 22(1): REG-17 with 7 working days to reply; rule 22(3): REG-19 within 30 days of the reply; rule 22(4) proviso: where cancellation was proposed under s.29(2)(b) or (c) and the person files all pending returns and pays dues with interest and late fee, "the proper officer **shall** drop the proceedings" (REG-20). Rule 21A(2): suspension where the officer has reasons to believe registration is liable to be cancelled. s.30(2) proviso and rule 23: revocation application within 90 days of service of the cancellation order, extendable by up to 180 days by the Commissioner or an authorised Additional/Joint Commissioner for reasons recorded; not rejected without a hearing. s.29(3): cancellation does not affect earlier liability.
- **Cases:**
  - Vague REG-17: *A1 Adil Traders* (04.03.2025; facts must be in the REG-17 itself, not only in an annexed letter), *Rayees Metals* (Telangana, **BINDING**); *Aggarwal Dyeing* (Gujarat), Delhi line, *PAV Warehouse* (Madras), *King Enterprises* (Karnataka), Gauhati (non-filing period not specified). Counterweight *Trillion Lead Factory* (Telangana, **BINDING**). [06]
  - Order on a different ground: *JK Technology* (Telangana, **BINDING**). [06]
  - No hearing / no hearing date: *Sheeshmahal* (Telangana, **BINDING**); *Suresh Trading* (Madras). [06]
  - No reasons in REG-19: Gauhati HC 2026. [06]
  - Retrospective date: *Ridhi Sidhi Enterprises*, W.P.(C) 8061/2024; *Aditya Polymers*; *Subhana Fashion*, W.P.(C) 12255/2024; *Aryan Timber Store*, W.P.(C) 628/2024 (Delhi HC): back-dating must be proposed in REG-17, rest on objective criteria and stated reasons, and weigh the effect on buyers' ITC; else the date moves to the SCN, suspension or application date. *JK Technology* consistent. No contrary view found. [06], [02]
  - Suspension: *El Tech Power System*, Madras HC, 10.03.2026 (automatic suspension in REG-17 unwarranted); *Shakti Shiva Magnets*, Delhi HC 2022; suspension falls with a vague SCN (*A1 Adil*, *Rayees*). [06]
  - Non-existence: *Umesh* (Delhi), *F R Trade Links* (Kerala) quashed; Patna HC W.P. 6794/2025 upheld where procedure followed. [06]
  - NIL returns are returns: *Snowline Travel Ventures*, Uttarakhand HC, 23.07.2026. [06]
  - Revocation after the time limit: Telangana HC 2026 (*Adarsha Transport*, *KBG Associates v. Superintendent of Central Tax, Secunderabad CGST Range*, *Check Security Force*): physical revocation application to be entertained and decided on merits within about 3 weeks. **BINDING IN TELANGANA** (as directions). *Suguna Cutpiece* (Madras), *Motaleb Bhuyan* (Gauhati): restoration once returns and dues are cleared. [06]
  - Refusal of fresh registration (REG-05) or of the taxpayer's own cancellation request: *Maharaj Ji Enterprises* (Patna; Circular 95/14/2019 findings needed in the order); *Om Impex* (Bombay). [06]
- **Fix (REG-17):** clause of s.29(2) → facts → periods or returns missed → inspection date, time, officer and findings (report attached) → proposed effective date and why → hearing date and time → reason for any suspension. **(REG-19):** decide only the proposed ground; deal with the reply; reasons for the effective date; if the person files returns and pays dues in a non-filing case, drop under the rule 22(4) proviso. **(Revocation):** decide on merits with reasons after a hearing; comply with any HC direction within the time it fixes.

**H10. Refund orders (short note; full treatment is in the refund templates)**
- **Grounds courts quash on:** rejection without RFD-08, reply or hearing (rule 92(3) and its proviso; *DSV Air and Sea*, Telangana) [01]; limitation raised through a deficiency memo instead of RFD-08 (*Pacific Wood Tech*, Madras HC, 01.09.2026) [01]; ITC eligibility decided inside a refund without a s.73/74 order (*Augustan Textile Colours*, GSTAT) [09]; Circular 135 para 3.2 applied to concessional-rate supplies (*Micro Systems*, Telangana, **BINDING**) [01]; withholding without the s.54(11) conditions (*ADV Industrial Services*, Telangana, **BINDING**) [01]; "intermediary" label without contract analysis (*Virtusa*, Telangana, **BINDING**) [01]; s.56 interest refused or made subject to a rule 89(2)(m) certificate (*Qualcom*, *Synchrony*, Telangana, **BINDING**) [01]; rule 96(10) used in a pending proceeding (*Goodluck India*, **SC**) [01].
