R1 REG-03 query; R2 verification report; R3 REG-17 with ground modules; R4 REG-19; R5 REG-20; R6 suspension reasons note and REG-31 record; R7 REG-23; R8 REG-22 or REG-05 on revocation; R9 REG-05 on applications; R10 checklist.

**Four things to know first**
1. **The Range Superintendent is the proper officer** for grant, amendment, cancellation, suspension and revocation (Circular 223/17/2024-GST, 10.07.2024). The AC approves pre-grant verification under rule 9(1) proviso (b) and REG-03 requests for documents outside the REG-01 list.
2. **Appeals against these orders go to an officer not below Joint Commissioner (Appeals)**, not to the Commissioner (Appeals) (rule 109A(1)(b)).
3. **A REG-17 must state facts, not just the clause**, and the REG-19 must decide only that ground (Telangana HC: *Rayees Metals*, *A1 Adil Traders*, *JK Technology*).
4. **A retrospective cancellation date must be proposed in the REG-17 and justified** (Delhi HC line); suspension needs a stated reason (Madras *El Tech Power*; Telangana *Rayees Metals*).
