## Page one: what matters most

**1. In the Central Tax administration, the proper officer for registration is the Superintendent, not the Assistant Commissioner.** Circular 223/17/2024-GST (10.07.2024) substituted the table in Circular 1/1/2017. The Superintendent of Central Tax is now the proper officer for s.25(8), the proviso to s.27(1), **s.28, s.29 and s.30**, and rules 6, 9, 10, 12, 16, 17, 19, **22, 23** and 25. The Assistant or Deputy Commissioner keeps only s.10(5) (composition penalty). Before 10.07.2024, s.30 and rules 23 and 25 sat with the AC/DC. Three consequences:
- Notices and orders on registration should be issued by the jurisdictional **Range Superintendent** on the back office. The templates in this module (R-series) are drafted for the Superintendent's signature.
- The AC/DC can still act under s.5(2) (an officer may exercise the powers of an officer subordinate to him), but then the order is the AC's order, so record that s.5(2) is used. Do not mix: the officer who hears must decide.
- **The appeal against a Superintendent's registration order lies to an officer not below the rank of Joint Commissioner (Appeals)** (rule 109A(1)(b)), not to the Commissioner (Appeals). The appeal paragraph in an order must say so.

**2. Three things the AC still does in registration work:**
- approves physical verification **before** grant, where the officer "deems it fit" (rule 9(1) proviso (b); Instruction 03/2025 para 8(iii)(c));
- approves any REG-03 query for a document **not in the REG-01 list** (Instruction 03/2025 para 8(vi));
- supervises the Range: deemed approvals, queries, verification reports and cancellations.

**3. Time limits the officer can miss** (see the table in Part 10): REG-03 within 7 working days of the application (30 days for risky, non-Aadhaar or verification cases); decision within 7 working days of the REG-04 reply; **otherwise registration is deemed granted (rule 9(5))**. REG-19 within 30 days of the REG-18 reply (rule 22(3)). REG-22 within 30 days of the REG-21 application (rule 23(2)(a)). REG-30 report at least 5 working days before the 30-day limit (rule 25(2)).

**4. The four defects that lose most registration cases in court** (all binding in Telangana): a REG-17 that only copies the words of s.29(2) or rule 21 (*Rayees Metals*, *A1 Adil Traders*); a REG-19 on a ground not in the REG-17 (*JK Technology*); a REG-19 that does not reflect the record or the reply (*Sheeshmahal*); cancellation from a back date not proposed or reasoned (Delhi HC line, followed in Telangana practice). Part 2 of the checklist file has the full list.

**5. Live points as on 14.10.2026:**
- Telangana is **excluded** from the ₹40 lakh threshold for goods (Notification 10/2019-CT, clause (iii)). The threshold in Telangana is **₹20 lakh** for goods and services alike.
- Rule 9A (system grant in 3 working days on risk parameters) and rule 14A (optional fast registration where output tax to registered buyers is up to ₹2.5 lakh a month; exit by REG-32) are in force from 01.11.2025 (Notification 18/2025-CT).
- The 57th Council (08.10.2026) recommended auto-accepted amendments (rule 19), removal of some rule 21 grounds, system-based cancellation and revocation for non-filing and missing bank details (rules 21A, 22, new 23A) and a new rule 14B. **Not notified yet.** Part 11 lists what to re-check.

## Part 1. Who must register, and who need not

### 1.1 Liability (s.22)
- Every supplier making taxable supplies from a State, when **aggregate turnover** in the financial year exceeds **₹20 lakh** (₹10 lakh in the special category States as now defined). Aggregate turnover is PAN-based and all-India (s.2(6)); it includes exempt and zero-rated supplies and supplies made on behalf of principals, and excludes the value of goods supplied by a registered job worker after job work (Explanation to s.22).
- The ₹40 lakh limit for persons supplying **only goods** (Notification 10/2019-CT, s.23(2)) **does not apply in Telangana**, and never applies to ice cream, pan masala and tobacco, to persons liable under s.24, or to voluntary registrants.
- Successor or transferee of a going concern: liable from the date of transfer (s.22(3)); on amalgamation or demerger under a court or NCLT order, from the date of the ROC certificate (s.22(4)).
- **Time to apply:** within 30 days of becoming liable (s.25(1)); casual and non-resident taxable persons at least five days before starting business.

### 1.2 Compulsory registration regardless of turnover (s.24)
Inter-State taxable suppliers; casual taxable persons; persons liable under reverse charge; e-commerce operators liable under s.9(5); non-resident taxable persons; TDS deductors (s.51); agents supplying on behalf of others; Input Service Distributors; suppliers through an e-commerce operator who collects TCS; every TCS e-commerce operator; overseas OIDAR suppliers to unregistered recipients; overseas online money gaming suppliers (s.24(xia), from 01.10.2023).

### 1.3 Exemptions from registration (s.23 and notifications under s.23(2))
| Category | Source |
|---|---|
| Persons dealing only in non-taxable or wholly exempt supplies; agriculturists for produce of their own cultivation | s.23(1) |
| Persons making only reverse-charge supplies (where the recipient pays the tax) | Notification 5/2017-CT |
| Suppliers of **services** making inter-State supplies, below the s.22 threshold | Notification 10/2017-IT |
| Job workers making inter-State supply of services to a registered person (conditions apply) | Notification 7/2017-IT |
| Inter-State supply of specified handicraft goods, and casual taxable persons supplying them, below the threshold | Notifications 8/2017-IT and 32/2017-CT |
| Suppliers of **services** through an e-commerce operator, below the threshold | Notification 65/2017-CT |
| Suppliers of **goods** through an e-commerce operator (intra-State only, conditions apply) | Notification 34/2023-CT (waives s.24(ix)) |

*Source note:* subjects of these notifications are from the CBIC index (`../data/notifications.csv`); only 10/2019-CT was read in full here. Read the text before relying on a condition.

### 1.4 Separate and special registrations
- One registration per State; separate registration for each place of business is optional (s.25(2), rule 11). Separate registrations are **distinct persons** (s.25(4), (5)).
- A **SEZ unit or developer** must take a separate registration from its business outside the SEZ in the same State (second proviso to s.25(1)).
- **ISD** needs a separate application (proviso to rule 8(1)).
- **TDS/TCS:** REG-07, granted in 3 working days (rule 12); cancelled by REG-08 when no longer liable, following rule 22 procedure (rule 12(3)).
- **Non-resident taxable person:** REG-09 with passport or foreign tax ID, advance deposit, authorised signatory resident in India with PAN (rule 13).
- **UIN** for UN bodies, embassies and notified persons: REG-13, in 3 working days (rule 17).
- **Voluntary registration:** all provisions apply (s.25(3)); cancellation possible if business not commenced within six months (s.29(2)(d)); a voluntary registrant may opt out (s.29(1)(c)).

### 1.5 Casual taxable persons (s.27, rule 15, Circular 71/45/2018)
- Valid for the period applied for or 90 days, whichever is earlier; extendable by up to 90 days by the proper officer (now the Superintendent) on REG-11 filed before expiry, with additional deposit.
- The advance deposit is the **estimated net tax liability** after eligible ITC, not gross tax (Circular 71/45/2018, issue 1).
- An exhibition running more than 180 days cannot be done as a casual taxable person; the person needs normal registration, with the allotment or consent letter as proof of the place of business (Circular 71/45/2018, issue 2).

## Part 3. New registration (s.25, rules 8 to 10)

### 3.1 The application
1. **Part A of REG-01:** PAN, State, mobile and e-mail, verified by OTPs sent to the mobile and e-mail **linked to the PAN** (rule 8(2)(a), from 26.12.2022). A temporary reference number (TRN) is generated.
2. **Part B of REG-01** with the documents listed in the form, signed by DSC or EVC (rule 8(4)).
3. **Aadhaar (rule 8(4A)):**
   - If the applicant opts for Aadhaar authentication, the date of submission is the date of authentication or 15 days from Part B, whichever is earlier.
   - If the portal flags the applicant on risk parameters, **biometric Aadhaar authentication, photograph and verification of original documents at a GST Suvidha Kendra** follow, and the application is complete only after that (first proviso).
   - If the applicant does **not** opt for Aadhaar, photograph and document verification at a Suvidha Kendra follow (second proviso, from 11.02.2025, Notification 12/2024-CT read with 09/2025-CT).
   - The provisos apply only in States notified under rule 8(4B). Notification 27/2022-CT as amended (last seen: 54/2023-CT) lists **Andhra Pradesh, Gujarat and Puducherry**. Whether **Telangana** was added by a later amendment was **not confirmed** in this round (see Gaps).
4. **Acknowledgement** REG-02 (rule 8(5)); for casual and non-resident persons only after the advance deposit (rules 8(6), 13(2)).

### 3.2 The officer's examination (rule 9; Instruction 03/2025)
**What to check** (Instruction 03/2025 para 8(i)):
- that the documents are legible, complete and relevant;
- that the details in the form match the documents, especially every address of the principal and additional places of business;
- where possible, the authenticity of the address proof from public sources (land registry, DISCOM, municipality websites);
- other GSTINs on the same PAN, and whether any is cancelled or suspended (Circular 95/14/2019; Instruction 03/2025 para 8(v)(d)).

**Documents you may ask for: only those in the REG-01 list** (Instruction 03/2025 para 6):

| Premises | Enough |
|---|---|
| Owned | **Any one** of: latest property tax receipt, municipal khata, electricity bill, water bill, or any other State or local document clearly showing ownership. No second document, no originals. |
| Rented, registered lease | Lease + any one ownership document of the lessor. **No identity proof of the lessor.** |
| Rented, unregistered lease | Lease + any one ownership document + identity proof of the lessor. |
| Rented, utility bill in the tenant's name | Lease + that bill. Nothing more about the lessor. |
| Owned by spouse or relative, no lease | Consent letter on plain paper + consenter's identity proof + any one ownership document. |
| Shared premises | As for rented (with lease) or as for consent (without lease). |
| Rented, no lease available | Affidavit on minimum-value non-judicial stamp paper before a Magistrate or Notary + a document showing possession (e.g. electricity bill in the applicant's name). |
| SEZ | Government of India documents or certificates for the SEZ unit or developer. |
| Partnership | Partnership deed. **Not** Udyam, MSME, shop-establishment or trade licence. |
| Society, trust, club, AOP, government body, local authority | Registration certificate or proof of constitution. |

**Queries that must not be raised** (Instruction 03/2025 para 7, 8(vi)): that the promoter's residence is in another city or State; that the HSN declared is "banned" in the State; that the activity cannot be carried on from the premises; any presumptive query not arising from the documents; queries on minor deficiencies that do not affect proof of place of business or constitution; lessor's PAN, Aadhaar or photograph in front of the property. **Strict action** against officers who deviate is directed (para 9(ii)).

**Grounds on which REG-03 may be issued** (Instruction 03/2025 para 8(v)): a document incomplete or illegible; address not matching the document, or the document not a valid address proof; address incomplete or vague; another GSTIN on the PAN cancelled or suspended. A fresh application by a person whose earlier registration was cancelled under s.29(2)(b) or (c), who has not sought revocation and whose default continues, is itself a "deficiency" under rule 9(2) (Circular 95/14/2019 para 4).

### 3.3 Time limits and outcomes
| Case | Officer must | By | Authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Not flagged risky, Aadhaar authenticated, no deficiency | Approve | **7 working days** from submission | Rule 9(1); Instr. 03/2025 para 8(ii) |
| Flagged risky after Aadhaar, or no Aadhaar, or officer chooses verification (with AC approval) | Physically verify (rule 25) and decide | **30 days** from submission | Rule 9(1) proviso |
| Deficiency or clarification needed | Issue REG-03 | **7 working days** (30 days in proviso cases) | Rule 9(2) |
| REG-04 reply received and satisfactory | Approve | **7 working days** from the reply | Rule 9(3) |
| No reply, or reply unsatisfactory | Reject by **REG-05, for reasons recorded in writing** | 7 working days from the reply, or from expiry of the reply time | Rule 9(4); Instr. 03/2025 para 8(vii)-(viii) |
| Officer does nothing within these periods | Registration is **deemed approved** | Automatic | Rule 9(5); s.25(12) |
| Applicant identified by the portal on risk parameters (rule 9A) | Portal grants electronically | 3 working days | Rule 9A (from 01.11.2025) |
| Applicant opts for rule 14A and authenticates Aadhaar | Portal grants electronically | 3 working days | Rule 14A(4) |

- "Clarification" in rule 9(2) includes correcting particulars in the form, except PAN, State, mobile and e-mail of Part A (Explanation to rule 9(2)).
- Instruction 03/2025 para 8(vi): **no application should be approved on deemed basis for want of timely action.** A deemed grant of a fake entity is a supervisory failure, and the only cure is cancellation proceedings later.
- REG-03 as substituted by Notification 18/2025-CT has a hearing line ("You are hereby directed to appear before the undersigned"), but it is **not applicable for new registration and withdrawal**; use it for amendment and cancellation applications only.

### 3.4 Certificate and effective date (rule 10)
- REG-06 is made available on the portal; GSTIN = 2 digits State code + PAN/TAN + entity code + check digit.
- Effective date: the date liability arose, if the application was made within 30 days; otherwise **the date of grant** (rule 10(2), (3)).
- Deemed grant: certificate within 3 days after the rule 9(5) period (rule 10(5)).
- **Bank account (rule 10A):** within 30 days of grant or before filing GSTR-1/IFF, whichever is earlier (except TDS/TCS and suo motu registrants). Failure is a ground for suspension by REG-31 (rule 21A(2A)(b)) and cancellation (rule 21(d)).
- **Aadhaar for existing registrants (rule 10B):** authentication of the proprietor, a partner, karta, MD or whole-time director, a committee member, trustee **and the authorised signatory** is a condition for filing REG-21 (revocation), RFD-01 (refund) and for IGST export refunds under rule 96. If no Aadhaar number is assigned: enrolment ID slip plus one photo ID, and authentication within 30 days of allotment.
- Display the certificate at every place of business and the GSTIN on the name board (rule 18). Absence is a fact worth recording in a verification report, but it is not by itself a ground under s.29(2) or rule 21.

### 3.5 Rule 14A (optional fast registration) and withdrawal
- Available to an applicant whose output tax on supplies **to registered persons** (CGST + SGST/UTGST + IGST + cess) will not exceed ₹2.5 lakh a month, who authenticates Aadhaar; one such registration per PAN per State (rule 14A(1)-(4)).
- To exceed the limit, the person files **REG-32**. Conditions: returns for at least one tax period (applications on or after 01.04.2026), all returns due till the application, no s.29 proceedings initiated, and particulars amended under rule 19 first (rule 14A(5), (6)).
- The application is verified as under rule 9; the officer issues **REG-33** (allowed) or **REG-05** (rejected) within the rule 9 periods. **If cancellation proceedings start after the REG-32 is filed and are pending, the REG-32 must be rejected and is not deemed approved** (rule 14A(13)).

### 3.6 Fresh application by a person whose earlier registration was cancelled (Circular 95/14/2019)
1. Fetch all registrations on the PAN and on the promoters (S. No. 21 of REG-01).
2. Find the ground of the earlier cancellation and whether revocation was sought.
3. If the earlier cancellation was under s.29(2)(b) or (c) (non-filing), no revocation was sought and the default continues, that is a deficiency under rule 9(2): issue REG-03 asking why revocation was not sought and whether the pending returns and dues have been cleared.
4. Reject by REG-05 only if the justification is not convincing, and **write the finding in the REG-05 itself** (Patna HC *Maharaj Ji Enterprises*, 2025: reasons given only in the counter-affidavit do not save the order).

## Part 4. Physical verification (rule 25, REG-30)

| | Before grant | After grant |
|---|---|---|
| When | Rule 9(1) proviso cases | Whenever the officer is satisfied it is required |
| Report | REG-30 with photographs | REG-30 with photographs |
| Upload by | **At least 5 working days before** the 30-day limit (rule 25(2)) | **Within 15 working days** of the visit (rule 25(1)) |

Since 04.08.2023 (Notification 38/2023-CT) the visit need not be "in the presence of" the applicant. Instruction 03/2025 para 8(iv) requires the verifying officer to:
- give a **specific** report on whether the declared principal place of business exists;
- if it does not, record the efforts made to locate it;
- upload **GPS-enabled photographs** and any documents;
- re-assign at once an ARN that belongs to another jurisdiction.

What courts look for in the report (see checklist ground R-D): date and time, names and designations of the officers, the exact address visited, who was met (name, relationship to the business), what was seen (signboard, stock, records, activity), what was asked and answered, photographs, and the source of any statement (no "neighbours said" from unnamed persons: Delhi HC *Umesh*, 2024). The officer who decides must apply his own mind to the report, not merely forward another wing's conclusion (Kerala HC *F R Trade Links*, 2021). Template **R2** gives the format.

## Part 5. Suo motu and temporary registration (s.25(8), rules 16 and 16A)
- When a survey, enquiry, inspection, search or other proceeding finds a person liable to register who has not, the officer may register him on a temporary basis by **REG-12**, effective from the date of the order (rule 16(1), (2)).
- The person must apply under rule 8 or 12 within **90 days** (or within 30 days of an appellate order upholding liability, if he appealed); the GSTIN then relates back to the REG-12 date (rule 16(3)-(5)).
- Registration is "without prejudice" to other action (s.25(8)); tax for the earlier period is assessed under s.63 (assessment of unregistered persons).
- Rule 16A (temporary identification number for a person not liable to register who must make a payment, Part B of REG-12) was inserted by Notification 07/2025-CT but **is not yet in force** per the CBIC text.

## Part 6. Amendment (s.28, rule 19)

| Change | Approval | Time | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Legal name; address of principal or additional place; partners, directors, karta, trustees, CEO (not warranting cancellation) | Proper officer approves by **REG-15** after verification | 15 working days | From the date of the event (rule 19(1)(a)) |
| Any other particular | None; certificate stands amended on filing REG-14 | Immediate | Rule 19(1)(c) |
| Change of constitution that changes the PAN | Fresh registration (REG-01) | — | Rule 19(1)(d) |
| Mobile or e-mail of the authorised signatory | Online OTP verification | — | Second proviso to rule 19(1) |
| Any amendment from a date earlier than the REG-14 | Only by order of the **Commissioner**, for recorded reasons | — | Rule 19(1A) |

- **To refuse an amendment** the officer must serve **REG-03** within 15 working days of the REG-14, giving 7 working days to show cause; on no reply or an unsatisfactory REG-04, reject by **REG-05** (rule 19(2)-(4)). The second proviso to s.28(2) requires **an opportunity of being heard** before rejection, so fix a hearing date in the REG-03 (the substituted form has a hearing line for amendments).
- If the officer does nothing within 15 working days of the REG-14, or within 7 working days of the reply, the amendment is **deemed approved** (rule 19(5)).
- A change of name or of partners/directors made in one State applies to all registrations on the PAN (rule 19(1)(b)).

## Part 7. Cancellation

### 7.1 On the taxpayer's application (s.29(1), rule 20, Circular 69/43/2018)
**Who may apply:** the registered person (other than TDS/TCS and UIN holders), or the legal heirs of a deceased proprietor (s.29(1); rule 22(5)).

**Grounds:** discontinuance or closure; transfer, amalgamation, merger, demerger, sale or lease; change of constitution changing the PAN; no longer liable (including a voluntary registrant opting out); death of the sole proprietor; any other reason stated (Circular 69/43/2018 para 2).

**Form and time:** REG-16 within 30 days of the event, with stock details and liability. The 30 days may be read liberally where the event was spread over time (Circular 69/43/2018 para 3). The registration is **deemed suspended** from the date of application or the date from which cancellation is sought, whichever is later (rule 21A(1)).

**The officer's decision** (Circular 69/43/2018 paras 5, 6):
- **Accept within 30 days** of the application, unless (a) the REG-16 is incomplete, or (b) in a transfer, merger or amalgamation, the transferee is not yet registered.
- Cancel by **REG-19 from the date sought** in REG-16. **The effective date cannot be earlier than the date of the application.**
- In cases (a) and (b), write to the applicant giving 7 working days; if no reply or an unsatisfactory reply, reject on the system **after an opportunity of hearing**, recording reasons in the dialog box. The current REG-03 and REG-05 include "Cancellation", so use them: REG-03 with hearing date, then a reasoned REG-05 (Bombay HC *Om Impex*, 2024: a REG-05 without reasons and without the documents relied on was quashed).
- Payment under s.29(5) is **not** a precondition for applying; it can be made with GSTR-10 (para 8).
- If the officer has grounds under s.29(2) as well (e.g. bogus invoicing), issue a REG-17 and decide both together; do not leave the application pending without action.

### 7.2 By the officer (s.29(2), rule 21)
**Grounds** (cancel only on a ground listed here; Delhi HC *Subhana Fashion*: non-payment of dues is not one):

| Ground | Provision | Facts the REG-17 must state |
|---|---|---|
| Contravention of prescribed provisions, i.e. a rule 21 ground | s.29(2)(a) read with rule 21 | The clause of rule 21 and the facts below |
| No business from the declared place | Rule 21(a) | Date(s) of verification, findings, REG-30 reference, photographs |
| Invoice or bill without supply | Rule 21(b) | Invoices (number, date, recipient GSTIN, value), evidence that no goods or services moved (e-way bills, transport, payments, stock) |
| Violation of s.171 (anti-profiteering) | Rule 21(c) | The NAA/CCI order |
| Bank account not furnished (rule 10A) | Rule 21(d) | Date of grant, 30-day date, portal status |
| ITC availed in violation of s.16 | Rule 21(e) | Suppliers, invoices, the s.16 condition failed (e.g. supplier non-existent), period, amount |
| GSTR-1/1A outward supplies exceed GSTR-3B | Rule 21(f) | Tax periods, figures from each return, difference |
| Rule 86B (1% cash) violated | Rule 21(g) | Months, liability, cash paid, why no exception applies |
| Returns not filed within 30 days of a revocation order | Rule 21(ga) | Revocation order date, returns due, status |
| Monthly filer: returns not filed for a continuous **six months** | Rule 21(h) | Each tax period and return not filed |
| Quarterly (QRMP) filer: returns not filed for **two** continuous tax periods | Rule 21(i) | Each quarter not filed |
| Composition taxpayer: annual return not filed beyond three months of its due date | s.29(2)(b) | The financial year, due date, date three months later |
| Other registrants: returns not filed for the prescribed continuous period | s.29(2)(c) read with rule 21(h)/(i) | As above |
| Voluntary registrant not commencing business within six months | s.29(2)(d) | Date of registration, evidence of no business |
| Registration obtained by fraud, wilful misstatement or suppression | s.29(2)(e) | **What** was false or suppressed in REG-01 (e.g. forged lease, fictitious promoter), with the document |

NIL returns are returns; a person filing NIL returns is not a non-filer (Uttarakhand HC *Snowline Travel Ventures*, 2026).

**Procedure (rule 22):**
1. **REG-17** where the officer "has reasons to believe" the registration is liable to be cancelled. Reply in **REG-18** within **7 working days** of service. The form has a line for a personal hearing date and time, and (since Notification 26/2022-CT) for "supportive documents attached". Fill both. The s.29(2) proviso makes a hearing mandatory before cancellation.
2. If suspending (rule 21A(2)), state the date of suspension in the note at the foot of REG-17, and record the reasons (Part 8).
3. **Drop** by **REG-20** if the reply is satisfactory (rule 22(4)). **Mandatory drop** where cancellation was proposed for non-filing (s.29(2)(b) or (c)) and the person, instead of replying, **files all pending returns and pays the tax with interest and late fee** (proviso to rule 22(4)).
4. **Cancel** by **REG-19 within 30 days** of the REG-18 reply (or of the reply to a REG-31), from a date determined by the officer, directing payment of arrears including the s.29(5) amount (rule 22(3)). The current REG-19 (Notification 26/2022-CT) has six alternative recitals (no reply / reply / no reply and no appearance / no reply but appearance / reply but no appearance / reply and appearance). Use the right one, then give **reasons**, the **effective date**, and the reminders about GSTR-10, pending returns and s.29(3).
5. **Service and record:** issue on the portal; e-mail the registered address; keep proof of service (Telangana HC *Sheeshmahal*: an order reciting a reply that was never filed was set aside as mechanical).

**Effective date.** The officer may cancel "from such date, including any retrospective date, as he may deem fit" (s.29(2)). The courts' conditions (checklist ground R-F):
- propose the retrospective date **in the REG-17**, with the reason;
- tie the date to an objective fact (date business stopped, date of non-existence found, first bogus invoice), not mechanically to 01.07.2017 or the registration date;
- weigh and record the effect on recipients' ITC;
- if none of this is done, courts move the date to the SCN or suspension date (Delhi HC *Ridhi Sidhi*, *Aditya Polymers*, *Subhana Fashion*, *Aryan Timber*).

For cancellation on the taxpayer's own application, the date sought (not earlier than the application) is the default (Circular 69/43/2018 para 5).

### 7.3 After cancellation
- **s.29(3):** cancellation does not affect liability for any earlier period, whether determined before or after cancellation (Telangana HC DB *Manikanta Electronics Services Center*, 19.01.2026, binding: proceedings and portal service against a cancelled registrant upheld).
- **s.29(5):** pay the ITC on inputs in stock, inputs in semi-finished and finished goods, and capital goods (credit reduced by prescribed percentage points, or tax on transaction value, whichever is higher) or the output tax on them, whichever is higher, as on the day before the effective date. Computed under rule 44; reported in GSTR-10. Ledger balances cannot be used after the effective date except for dues up to GSTR-10 (Circular 69/43/2018 para 8).
- **Final return GSTR-10** within three months of the effective date or the order date, whichever is later (s.45). If not filed: GSTR-3A notice; if still not filed within 15 days, best-judgment assessment by **ASMT-13** under s.62; withdrawn if GSTR-10 is filed within 30 days of the ASMT-13, but interest and late fee remain (Circular 69/43/2018 para 9).
- **Recipients:** where cancellation is for non-existence or bogus invoicing, list the recipients from GSTR-1, act on those in your jurisdiction, and send the rest to their administrations through the nodal officer (Instruction 02/2024 para 2(c)). Consider rule 86A blocking and s.83 attachment where revenue is at risk (para 2(c)(ii), (viii)). A supplier's retrospective cancellation does not by itself defeat a recipient's ITC; a separate s.73/74 case with evidence is needed (`../case-law/02-input-tax-credit.md`, Issue 8).

## Part 8. Suspension (s.29(1) and (2) provisos, rule 21A)

| Route | Trigger | From | Officer's action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rule 21A(1) | Taxpayer files REG-16 | Later of application date and date sought | Decide the REG-16 within 30 days |
| Rule 21A(2) | Officer **has reasons to believe** the registration is liable to cancellation under s.29 or rule 21 | A date the officer determines | Note on REG-17; record reasons; decide quickly |
| Rule 21A(2A)(a) | System comparison of GSTR-3B with GSTR-1 or with suppliers' GSTR-1 (or other analysis recommended by the Council) shows significant differences or anomalies | Date of the REG-31 | Examine reply within 30 days; REG-20 or REG-19 |
| Rule 21A(2A)(b) | Bank account not furnished (rule 10A) | Date of the REG-31 | Deemed revoked once details are furnished (third proviso to rule 21A(4)) |

**REG-31** is system-generated and unsigned. As substituted by Notification 20/2024-CT it has three variants: anomalies; rule 10A; non-filing under rule 21(h)/(i). The taxpayer replies to the jurisdictional officer within 30 days (Circular 145/01/2021 para 4). The task then appears on the officer's dashboard under "suo moto cancellation proceeding" (para 5.1).

**What the officer does with a REG-31** (Circular 145/01/2021 paras 5.2-5.4):
- examine the reply;
- pass **REG-20** (drop, and revoke suspension) or **REG-19** (cancel);
- if prima facie satisfied, revoke the suspension by REG-20 and continue detailed verification. If verification later shows the registration is liable to cancellation, start afresh with a REG-17.

**Deemed revocation of suspension:**
- REG-31 issued for non-filing: on furnishing all pending returns, if not already cancelled (second proviso to rule 21A(4)).
- REG-31 issued for rule 10A: on compliance (third proviso).
- In all cases: on completion of the rule 22 proceedings, with effect from the date the suspension began (rule 21A(4)).

The officer may revoke a suspension at any time during the proceedings (first proviso to rule 21A(4)).

**Effects of suspension:**
- no taxable supply, meaning no tax invoice and no tax charged (rule 21A(3) and Explanation);
- no return under s.39 during suspension;
- **no refund under s.54** while suspended under rule 21A(2) or (2A) (rule 21A(3A));
- when suspension is revoked, invoices for the period may be issued within 30 days of the revocation (s.31(3)(a)), and those supplies go into the first return after revocation (s.40) (rule 21A(5)).

**Hearing before suspension:** the words "after affording the said person a reasonable opportunity of being heard" were omitted from rule 21A(2) by Notification 94/2020-CT. Suspension without a prior hearing is lawful on the text, but courts quash suspensions that are automatic or have no stated basis (Madras HC *El Tech Power System*, 10.03.2026; Telangana HC *Rayees Metals*, deprecating mechanical suspension). A suspension falls with a vague REG-17 (*A1 Adil Traders*). So: suspend only where the REG-17 itself shows why business must stop pending decision (non-existence found, fake invoicing, continuing ITC passing). Write that reason in the REG-17 annexure. Then decide the REG-17 quickly. Template **R6** gives the recorded-reasons note.

## Part 9. Revocation of cancellation (s.30, rule 23)

**Who may apply:** a person whose registration was cancelled **by the proper officer on his own motion**, not one cancelled on his own application (s.30(1); rule 23(1)).

**Conditions to file REG-21:**
1. **Within 90 days** of service of the cancellation order (rule 23(1), from 01.10.2023; earlier 30 days).
2. **Extension** of up to **180 days** more, by the Commissioner or an officer authorised by him not below Additional or Joint Commissioner, on sufficient cause, for recorded reasons (first proviso). The old two-step 30+30 day extension and the SOP in Circulars 148/04/2021 and 158/14/2021 belong to the pre-01.10.2023 regime: read them for history and for COVID-period cases under Notification 34/2021-CT.
3. If cancelled for **non-filing**: all returns furnished and all tax due under them paid with interest, penalty and late fee **before** applying (second proviso).
4. **Aadhaar authentication** under rule 10B.

**After revocation** (third and fourth provisos to rule 23(1)):
- returns for the period from the date of the cancellation order to the revocation order must be filed within **30 days** of the revocation order;
- if cancellation was retrospective, returns from the effective date of cancellation onwards must be filed within the same 30 days;
- failure is itself a ground for cancellation (rule 21(ga)).

**Decision (rule 23(2)-(4)):**

| Outcome | Form | Time | Requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revoke | **REG-22** | **30 days** from the application | Satisfied, **for reasons recorded in writing** |
| Propose rejection | **REG-23** SCN; reply in REG-24 within **7 working days** | Before rejecting | Opportunity of hearing (proviso to s.30(2)); REG-23 has a hearing line |
| Reject | **REG-05** | 30 days from the REG-24 reply | Reasons recorded in writing (rule 23(2)(b)) |

The second proviso to s.30(2) (Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024) makes revocation subject to prescribed conditions and restrictions. No new conditions were seen in rule 23 beyond those above as on 12.10.2026.

**Applications after the time limit, under a High Court direction.** The portal stops a REG-21 after 270 days (90 + 180). Telangana HC Division Benches in 2026 repeatedly allowed a physical application after the limit, where the only ground was non-filing and the appeal was time-barred, and directed the authority to "entertain it and take a decision thereon in accordance with law within three weeks". Cases: *KBG Associates* v. Superintendent of Central Tax, Secunderabad CGST Range, 23.07.2026; *Check Security Force*, 16.07.2026; *Adarsha Transport*, 22.09.2026. When such an application arrives:
1. diarise the court's deadline;
2. verify returns and payment;
3. decide on merits by a speaking REG-22, or REG-23 then REG-05;
4. record that revocation does not wipe out liabilities (s.29(3)).

Gauhati HC (*Motaleb Bhuyan*) adds that s.73(10) limitation restarts from restoration. That is not binding here, but note it if a restoration order says so. Template **R8** covers both outcomes.

**Revocation versus appeal:** the taxpayer may appeal the REG-19 under s.107 (3 months + 1 month condonable) instead of or after revocation. A rejected REG-21 is itself appealable.
