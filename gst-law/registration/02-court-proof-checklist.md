# Court-proof checklist for registration notices and orders

**For:** Range Superintendents and the Assistant Commissioner, Sangareddy CGST Division (Medchal Commissionerate, Telangana).
**Compiled:** 14 Oct 2026.
**Built from:** `../case-law/06-registration-rectification.md` and `../case-law/05-telangana-high-court.md` (section 8), cited below as [06] and [05]. Statutory points were checked against the CBIC text of ss.25 to 30 and rules 9 to 25, Circulars 69/43/2018, 95/14/2019, 145/01/2021 and 223/17/2024, Instruction 03/2025 and the current REG forms (see [01-registration-procedure.md](01-registration-procedure.md)).

> **Reliability.** The Telangana HC orders cited here were read in full on Indian Kanoon by the case-law thread on 14 Oct 2026 ("Primary"). So were *Brindavan Beverages*, *Aggarwal Dyeing*, *Rayees Metals*, *A1 Adil Traders*, *JK Technology*, *Sheeshmahal*, *Trillion Lead Factory*, *Manikanta Electronics*, *KBG Associates*, *Check Security Force* and *Adarsha Transport*. Every other entry is "Secondary": it comes from search summaries. **Read the judgment before quoting it in a notice or order.** That thread is still verifying, so check [06] for later corrections.

**How to read each ground**
- **Defect:** what went wrong.
- **Cases:** court, case, how far it binds.
- **Result:** quashed, remanded, date modified, or relief refused.
- **Fix:** what to write or do.

**Binding:** **SC** binds everyone. **BINDING IN TELANGANA** = Telangana High Court. Other High Courts are persuasive, but the Joint Commissioner (Appeals) and GSTAT Hyderabad will read them.

## Part 1. Checks before you sign

Every answer should be **Yes**. A "No" points to the ground in Part 2.

### 1A. REG-03 query on a new registration application (12 checks)
| # | Check | Ground |
|---|---|---|

| 3 | Each query falls under one of the four permitted grounds in Instruction 03/2025 para 8(v): document incomplete or illegible; address not matching; address vague; another GSTIN on the PAN cancelled or suspended. | R-H2 |

| 8 | Each query says exactly what is wrong and exactly what will cure it, so the applicant can reply once. | R-H2 |
| 9 | If an earlier GSTIN on the PAN was cancelled for non-filing, I ask why revocation was not sought and whether returns and dues are now cleared (Circular 95/14/2019). | R-H3 |

## Part 2. The grounds

### A. Jurisdiction and authority

**R-A1. Wrong officer.**
- *Defect:* the notice or order is issued by an officer who is not the proper officer, or for a GSTIN allotted to the other administration.
- *Law:* Circular 1/1/2017 as amended by Circular 223/17/2024 (Superintendent for ss.28-30 and rules 9, 19, 22, 23, 25); s.5(2) (a superior officer may act); s.26 and ss.29(4), 30(3) (one administration's action deemed for the other).
- *Cases:* no registration-specific Telangana ruling was found. The general rule that an order without jurisdiction is void is in the adjudication checklist (`../adjudication/03-court-proof-checklist.md`, ground A2).
- *Fix:* sign as Superintendent of the Range. If the AC signs, say "exercising the powers of the proper officer under section 5(2)". Check the GSTIN's allotment before acting.

**R-A2. Parallel or duplicate proceedings.**
- *Defect:* a second REG-17 on the same ground while the first is pending, or a Central notice while the State administration (as allottee) is acting.
- *Fix:* one proceeding per ground; if new facts arise, issue an addendum to the pending REG-17 and give a fresh reply period.

**R-A3. Wrong appellate authority in the order.**
- *Defect:* the order tells the taxpayer to appeal to the Commissioner (Appeals), but rule 109A(1)(b) sends appeals against Superintendent, AC and DC orders to an officer not below Joint Commissioner (Appeals). The time limit then gets argued.
- *Fix:* name the right authority, the 3-month limit (s.107(1)), the 1-month condonation (s.107(4)) and APL-01 on the portal.

### B. The notice: content

**R-B1. Vague REG-17 that only repeats the statute.**
- *Defect:* the notice only says "registration obtained by fraud, wilful misstatement or suppression of facts", or lists rule 21 clauses, with no facts.
- *Cases:*
  - *Rayees Metals* v. DSTO, W.P. 17400/2024, 08.07.2024 [06] (**BINDING IN TELANGANA**): REG-17 with suspension, whose only "reason" was the text of s.29(2)(e), held cryptic. The Court deprecated mechanical suspensions. SCN and suspension set aside.
  - *A1 Adil Traders* v. DSTO, W.P. 4539/2025, 04.03.2025 [06] (**BINDING IN TELANGANA**): only rule 21(e), (b), (a) and s.29(2)(e) were listed. These were "conclusions", not reasons. Quashed with liberty to proceed afresh.
  - *Brindavan Beverages* (SC 2007) [06] (**SC**): the SCN is the foundation; vague allegations mean no proper opportunity.
  - Gujarat *Aggarwal Dyeing* (2022), Delhi W.P.(C) 10421/2023, Madras *PAV Warehouse*, Karnataka *King Enterprises* [06]: persuasive.
- *Result:* SCN, suspension and order quashed; restoration; liberty to issue a fresh notice.
- *Fix:* use the R3 modules. For each ground, state the clause, the facts with dates, periods, documents and figures, and the evidence.

**R-B2. Facts left in an annexed internal letter or another wing's report.**
- *Defect:* the REG-17 says "as per the DGGI letter" or "see enclosed report", and the reasons are found only there.
- *Cases:*
  - *A1 Adil Traders* [06] (**BINDING IN TELANGANA**): the assessee is not expected to dig reasons out of an annexed departmental letter.
  - *Trillion Lead Factory* v. Superintendent of Central Tax, Shamshabad, W.P. 537/2026, 02.02.2026 [05] (**BINDING IN TELANGANA**, SLP dismissed 27.02.2026): **no** relief where the DGGI communication was annexed and the petitioner replied in detail, so no prejudice.
  - Gujarat *Aggarwal Dyeing* [06]: any document relied on must first be put to the dealer.
- *Result:* depends on prejudice. The two Telangana rulings pull in different directions.
- *Fix:* write the essential facts into the REG-17 annexure itself, **and** attach the source document. In the REG-19, record that the reply addressed each allegation (it shows no prejudice).

**R-B3. A ground that is not in the statute, or a non-filing ground that does not fit.**
- *Defect:* cancellation for "non-payment of dues", for filing NIL returns, or for non-filing without naming the periods or without meeting the continuous period.
- *Cases:* Delhi *Subhana Fashion*, W.P.(C) 12255/2024 [06] (non-payment of dues is not a ground); Uttarakhand *Snowline Travel Ventures*, 2026 [06] (NIL returns are not non-filing); Gauhati HC 2026 [06] (non-filing SCN without the period is inadequate).
- *Fix:* list each tax period and return. Check the rule 21(h) or (i) threshold for the filer's frequency. Count NIL returns as returns.

**R-B4. Order on a ground not in the notice.**
- *Defect:* REG-17 under s.29(2)(e); REG-19 under s.29(2)(c).
- *Cases:* *JK Technology* v. DSTO Charminar, W.P. 21690/2026, 09.07.2026 [05] (**BINDING IN TELANGANA**): the order was quashed "for the sole reason that it is based upon a ground upon which the petitioner was never put to notice". The SCN was also quashed, the registration restored, and liberty given for a fresh notice.
- *Fix:* decide only the proposed ground. For a new ground, issue an addendum REG-17 and hear again.

### C. Hearing, reply and the order

**R-C1. Reply time cut short.**
- *Defect:* fewer than 7 working days from **service** (rule 22(1)), or deciding before the time runs out.
- *Fix:* count working days from service; do not decide before the reply date and the hearing.

**R-C2. No hearing date in the notice.**
- *Defect:* the REG-17 leaves the hearing line blank.
- *Cases:* Madras *Suresh Trading Corporation* [06] (cancellation quashed); proviso to s.29(2) (no cancellation without an opportunity of being heard).
- *Fix:* fill in the date, time and place or video link; if the taxpayer asks, adjourn once and record it.

**R-C3. Order that does not reflect the record; service not proved.**
- *Defect:* the REG-19 recites a reply that was never filed, or uses the wrong recital; the taxpayer swears the notice was never received.
- *Cases:* *Sheeshmahal Enterprises* v. Superintendent of Central Tax, W.P. 8344/2023, 28.03.2023 [05] (**BINDING IN TELANGANA**): the order cited a "reply dated 18.03.2021" that the petitioner said it never filed. Held mechanical, and remanded. Also *Manikanta Electronics* [06] (**BINDING IN TELANGANA**): portal service plus reminders and hearing notices upheld.
- *Fix:* pick the true recital from the six in REG-19. Record the service mode and date. Send an e-mail copy and keep proof.

**R-C4. Ignoring compliance made after the notice (non-filing cases).**
- *Defect:* cancelling although all returns were filed and dues paid before the order.
- *Law:* proviso to rule 22(4): the officer **shall** drop the proceedings (REG-20).
- *Cases:* the Madras *Suguna Cutpiece* line, Gauhati *Motaleb Bhuyan*, Telangana 2026 series [06]: courts restore once returns are filed and dues paid.
- *Fix:* check the portal on the day of the order; drop by REG-20 where the proviso applies.

**R-C5. Delay beyond 30 days.**
- *Defect:* REG-19 passed long after the reply, often with suspension running throughout.
- *Fix:* decide within 30 days (rule 22(3)). Courts criticise prolonged suspension, and suspension is meant to last only till the decision (Madras *El Tech Power*, 2026 [06]).

**R-C6. Non-speaking order.**
- *Defect:* the REG-19 copies the REG-17, or says "reply not satisfactory" without reasons.
- *Cases:* Gauhati HC 2026 (reasons must be in REG-19 itself) [06]; Delhi 2023-24 line ("equally cryptic" orders) [06]; Bombay *Om Impex* (REG-05 without reasons) [06].
- *Fix:* a findings table: each allegation, the reply, the evidence, the finding and its reason (template R4).

**R-C7. Different officer hears and decides.**
- *Fix:* the officer who heard signs. If the officer changed, offer a fresh hearing.

### D. Physical verification

**R-D1. Vague or undated verification report.**
- *Cases:* Delhi DB, Sept 2024 [06] (no verification date and no material showing non-existence: order quashed); Delhi *Umesh*, W.P.(C) 831/2024 [06] (the report relied on unnamed shopkeepers; a fresh inspection by the court found the premises).
- *Department wins when it is done right:* Patna W.P. 6794/2025 [06] (premises non-existent on repeated inspection, books not produced, procedure followed; no writ relief).
- *Fix:* the R2 format: date, time, officers, address, GPS photographs, persons met, observations, efforts to locate, conclusion. Attach to the REG-17.

**R-D2. The deciding officer did not apply his own mind; hearsay.**
- *Cases:* Kerala *F R Trade Links*, 2021 [06] (cancellation on an intelligence officer's report, without the registering officer's own rule 25 enquiry and without considering the building-tax receipt produced: set aside).
- *Fix:* the Superintendent records in the REG-19 what he examined: the report, its photographs, and the taxpayer's documents. If the report came from another wing, the Superintendent arranges his own verification or at least confronts the taxpayer with it.

**R-D3. Report not uploaded in time.**
- *Law:* rule 25 (15 working days after the visit; or 5 working days before the 30-day limit for pre-grant verification). A late pre-grant report risks deemed approval under rule 9(5).
- *Fix:* diarise both dates.

### E. Suspension

**R-E1. Mechanical suspension.**
- *Cases:* Madras *El Tech Power System*, W.P. 9579/2026, 10.03.2026 [06] (suspension in the REG-17 held unwarranted; notice quashed to that extent); Delhi *Shakti Shiva Magnets* (2022) [06]; Telangana *Rayees Metals* and *A1 Adil Traders* (suspension falls with a vague SCN) [06].
- *Law:* rule 21A(2) needs "reasons to believe" that the registration is liable to cancellation. No prior hearing is required since Notification 94/2020-CT (a Bombay HC challenge, *SAT Industries*, outcome not found).
- *Fix:* suspend only with reasons written in the REG-17 showing why business must stop now (e.g. non-existence, fake invoicing, continuing ITC passing). Keep the R6 note on file. Revoke by REG-20 if the reply is prima facie satisfactory (Circular 145/01/2021 para 5.4).

**R-E2. Suspension left running.**
- *Fix:* decide quickly. On any outcome, the order states that the suspension ends, and from when (rule 21A(4)).

### F. Effective date

**R-F1. Retrospective cancellation without proposal or reasons.**
- *Cases (all Delhi HC, persuasive; no contrary HC found; Telangana *JK Technology* did not rule on the date):* *Ridhi Sidhi Enterprises*, W.P.(C) 8061/2024 (power alone does not justify use; reasons must appear in the order); *Aditya Polymers* (SCN of 11.12.2020 did not propose retrospective cancellation; order back to 01.07.2017 modified to 11.12.2020); *Subhana Fashion* (date moved to the date of suspension); Delhi DB 2023-24 (objective criteria; date of discontinuance) [06].
- *Result:* the effective date is modified to the SCN date, the suspension date or the date of application.
- *Fix:* propose the date in the REG-17 with its objective basis (date business ceased; date of non-existence found; date of the first bogus invoice). Justify it in the REG-19.

**R-F2. Effect on recipients not considered.**
- *Cases:* Delhi *Aryan Timber Store*, W.P.(C) 628/2024 [02, Issue 8] (the officer must weigh the effect on customers' ITC; cancellation modified to the date of application).
- *Fix:* add a sentence in the REG-19 on why the chosen date is proportionate despite the effect on recipients (or why that effect is intended, e.g. invoices without supply).

### G. Cancellation on request and after-effects

**R-G1. Application left pending, or cancelled from a different date without reasons.**
- *Law:* Circular 69/43/2018 paras 5-6: accept within 30 days, effective from the date sought, never earlier than the application. Reject only if REG-16 is incomplete or the transferee is unregistered, after 7 working days and a hearing.
- *Cases:* Delhi orders moving dates to the application date [06]; Bombay *Om Impex* [06].
- *Fix:* decide REG-16s within 30 days (R4 has an "on application" variant). If you also have s.29(2) grounds, issue a REG-17 and decide both together.

**R-G2. Fresh registration refused without findings.** See R-H3.

**R-G3. Treating cancellation as ending liability, or as a substitute for a demand.**
- *Cases:* *Manikanta Electronics Services Center*, W.P. 1362/2026, 19.01.2026 [06] (**BINDING IN TELANGANA**): cancellation does not absolve existing liability (s.29(3)).
- *Fix:* REG-19 and REG-22 recite s.29(3). Tax, interest and penalty are demanded under s.73, 74 or 74A, not in the registration order (other than the rule 22(3) direction to pay arrears and the s.29(5) amount).

### H. Grant, query and rejection of new registration and amendment

**R-H1. Missed time limits leading to deemed grant.**
- *Law:* rules 9(1)-(5); Instruction 03/2025 para 8(vi) ("no application ... approved on deemed basis for want of timely action").
- *Fix:* diarise 7 working days / 30 days / 7 working days after the reply.

**R-H2. Unwarranted documents or presumptive queries.**
- *Law:* Instruction 03/2025 paras 6, 7, 8(v)-(vi); grievances go to the Chief Commissioner (Instruction 04/2025); "strict action" against deviating officers (para 9(ii)).
- *Cases:* no registration-grant ruling found in Telangana [06, Gaps]. Bombay *Om Impex* (reasons and documents) is the nearest.
- *Fix:* R1 template restricts queries to the permitted grounds.

**R-H3. Refusal without reasons in the order; Circular 95/14/2019 not applied.**
- *Cases:* Patna *Maharaj Ji Enterprises*, W.P. 15175/2024, July 2025 [06] (earlier cancellation no automatic bar; findings under s.29(2)(b)/(c) must be in the order; reasons in the counter-affidavit do not count; remanded).
- *Fix:* R9 template: findings on the earlier cancellation, revocation and continuing default, written in the REG-05.

**R-H4. REG-05 without a prior REG-03 or hearing.**
- *Law:* rule 9(4) (after REG-03); rule 19(2)-(4) and the second proviso to s.28(2) (amendment: hearing); Circular 69/43/2018 para 6 (cancellation application: hearing).
- *Fix:* REG-03 with specific points; REG-05 answering each.

### I. Revocation

**R-I1. Wrong time computation.**
- *Law:* 90 days from **service** of the REG-19, plus up to 180 days by the Commissioner or an authorised Addl./Joint Commissioner (rule 23(1), from 01.10.2023). Only for suo motu cancellations.
- *Fix:* record the service date and the computation in the order.

**R-I2. Revocation granted without the statutory conditions.**
- *Law:* returns and dues for non-filing cases (second proviso to rule 23(1)); rule 10B Aadhaar.
- *Fix:* verify the ledgers and returns; record them in REG-22.

**R-I3. Refusing a High Court-directed application only on time.**
- *Cases:* *KBG Associates* v. Superintendent of Central Tax, Secunderabad CGST Range, W.P. 23897/2026, 23.07.2026 [06] (**BINDING IN TELANGANA**, a CGST Range); *Check Security Force*, W.P. 22632/2026 [06]; *Adarsha Transport*, W.P. 31578/2026 [06]: physical application allowed after the portal lock; to be decided "in accordance with law within three weeks". Also the Madras *Suguna* and Gauhati *Motaleb Bhuyan* lines (persuasive).
