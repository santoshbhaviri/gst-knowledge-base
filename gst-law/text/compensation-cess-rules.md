# Goods and Services Tax Compensation Cess Rules, 2017

**Source:** CBIC Tax Information Portal, https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content-page/explore-rules/1000029 (section-wise text as published there, downloaded 12 Oct 2026).
**How to read it:** numbers in square brackets mark amended text; the footnotes after each section name the amending Act or notification. This is a working copy for search; cite the CBIC page for anything formal.

### Rule 1. Short title and commencement

Rule 1. Short title and commencement. (1) These rules may be called the Goods and Services Tax Compensation Cess Rules, 2017. (2) They shall come into force with effect from the 1st day of July, 2017.

### Rule 2. Adaptation of Central Goods and Services Tax Rules, 2017

Rule 2. Adaptation of Central Goods and Services Tax Rules, 2017.- (1) The Central Goods and Services Tax Rules, 2017 shall, mutatis mutandis, apply, subject to the following modifications, namely:- (a) in rule 1,- (i) for the words and figures "Central Goods and Services Tax Rules, 2017", the words and figures, "Goods and Services Tax Compensation Cess Rules, 2017" shall be substituted; (b) rules 3 to 7shall be omitted; (c) rules 117 to 120 shall be omitted.
