# GST Case Law Compendium

**Sangareddy CGST Division · compiled 14 Oct 2026 · {N} judgments across all GST domains**

All judgments in one place: Supreme Court, High Courts (Telangana HC flagged as binding), GST Appellate Tribunal, appellate authorities and advance rulings, organised by domain, with the points on which courts disagree. It is part of the project's GST knowledge base (`/mnt/project-files/gst-law/`), alongside the Acts, Rules, notifications, circulars and Council minutes.

## Part 1. How to use this compendium

**Reliability.** On 14-15 Oct 2026 every entry was checked against the judgment text on Indian Kanoon (and the Telangana HC and SC sites). An entry marked *Primary - read on Indian Kanoon* has been read at its operative part; its first source link is the judgment, and a "Key words" line quotes the holding with the paragraph number. Entries marked *Secondary* (mostly GSTAT orders, advance rulings and very recent interim orders, which are not on Indian Kanoon) are still from reports only: **read those before quoting them.** Entries marked **[DOUBTFUL]** or "do not cite" should not be relied on. Each domain file ends with a verification log listing what was corrected. Each entry gives the citation, holding, binding status, later history (appeal, SLP, reversal, or overtaken by amendment), a one-line "use in orders" note, source links and its verification status.

**What binds you.**
- **Supreme Court:** binding on all authorities (Art. 141). This compendium has {NSC} SC entries.
- **Telangana High Court:** binding on officers in Telangana; a lower authority must follow its jurisdictional High Court (*Union of India v. Kamlakshi Finance*, SC). This compendium has {NTG} entries marked BINDING IN TELANGANA.
- **Other High Courts:** persuasive. Where they conflict and the SC and Telangana HC have not spoken, follow the CBIC position and record the conflict in the order.
- **GSTAT:** followed by the appellate and adjudicating authorities below it; a High Court or SC ruling prevails over it.
- **AAR/AAAR:** binding only on the applicant and its jurisdictional officer (s.103).
- **CBIC circulars:** bind officers, not courts or taxpayers (*Ratan Melting & Wire*, SC).

**Structure.** Part 2 lists where courts disagree and what is pending in the Supreme Court. Part 3 is a one-line index of every judgment by domain. Part 4 has the full entries, with "Quick answers" for each issue at the top of each file. The same entries are in `case-law-index.csv` for filtering in Excel.

**Recent rulings that change drafting.**
- *G.R. Infra Projects* (SC, 19.08.2026): a s.74 SCN must state the specific facts of fraud or suppression in the notice itself.
- *Sterling & Wilson* (GSTAT Principal Bench, 11.02.2026; order not yet read, from reports): a return mismatch alone does not support s.74; re-working under s.75(2) is for the proper officer.
- *Armour Security* (SC, 14.08.2025): a summons or search is not "initiation of proceedings" under s.6(2)(b); an SCN on the same subject matter is.
- *Goodluck India* (SC, 06.08.2026): omission of rule 96(10) applies to all proceedings pending on 08.10.2024.
- *Gudla Vanaja* (**Telangana HC Full Bench, 30.09.2026**, W.P. 20397/2025 batch): an SCN or order electronically authenticated and uploaded on the portal is valid without a visible signature, but **a notice or order without a DIN/RFN is invalid**; limitation runs from upload. It overrules *Bigleap Technologies* (2025), which must no longer be cited.
- *Devi Electronics* (Delhi HC, 01.10.2026): a refund may be withheld under s.54(11) where an anti-evasion investigation is already pending; no court stay is needed.
- *A1 Adil Traders* (Telangana HC, 04.03.2025): the grounds must be stated in the SCN itself, not only in an annexed letter.
- *Augustan Textile Colours* (GSTAT Ernakulam, 2026; from reports): ITC eligibility cannot be decided inside a refund proceeding without a s.73/74 order.
