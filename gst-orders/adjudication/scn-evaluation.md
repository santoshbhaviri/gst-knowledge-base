## Act on these first
1. **S4 and S9, mining valuation.** Both multiply the per-tonne price by 12.5 to get a price per square metre. The SCNs take 12.5 from the seigniorage rates (₹8 per m² against ₹100 per tonne). That ratio means 1 m² is treated as 0.08 tonne, so the price per m² should be the per-tonne price **divided** by 12.5, not multiplied. The value is overstated about 156 times (12.5 × 12.5). I checked this myself against both SCNs' tables. Corrected, each year may fall below the registration threshold. **Get the conversion basis from the Mines department before adjudicating.** A corrigendum that adds tax is not possible once limitation has passed, but one that reduces the demand can be issued, or the order can drop the excess.
2. **S8 limitation.** It was signed 29.09.2026, one day before the s.73(2) bar for FY 2022-23. Confirm today the DRC-01 date on the portal.
3. **S10 "DRAFT".** Confirm that a final notice was issued by 28.08.2026, the bar for FY 2020-21. Otherwise that year falls.
4. **S1 FY 2021-22** s.73 items are time-barred and should be dropped in the order.
5. **Order deadlines:**
   - **By 31.12.2026:** FY 2022-23 items under s.73 (S1 para 3.1, S2, S5, S6, S7, S8, S10 issue (iv)).
   - **By 28.02.2027:** FY 2020-21 items under s.74 (S3, S9, S10, S11).

## Recurring defects (how many of 11)
1. **Hearing:** no date, time or venue (11). Most say the hearing is waived unless asked for, contrary to s.75(4) (the primary authority; *B.K. Steels*, Telangana HC, applied it, but by consent, so its precedent weight is limited).
2. **Limitation:** not computed in any SCN (11). Most were issued 1 to 8 days before the bar, which leaves no room for a corrigendum.
3. **Interest:** not quantified or no sub-section given (10). s.50(3) used without showing utilisation, and the s.50(1) cash-portion proviso not applied.
4. **Penalty:** pleaded as "s.122(2) read with s.73/74" instead of s.73(9), s.74(1) or s.74A(5) (10). Overlapping penalties appear in S4 and S9.
5. **Relied-upon documents:** described as "already available", with no annexure (11). Other wings' data (DG Audit, mines, e-way bill) was not supplied.
6. **Standard paragraphs missing:** limitation dates, State-overlap check (s.6), the officer's authority under Circular 31/254 limits, and the portal upload and service record.
7. **Several years in one SCN** (S1, S3, S4, S9, S10). In Telangana a composite multi-year s.74 SCN is lawful (*Goldstone Infra*, Telangana HC DB, 27.01.2026), so this is not a legal defect by itself. One SCN per year is still recommended as safer practice: separate limitation for each year and cleaner orders. The real defects are missing year-wise figures and limitation, and mixing s.74A with s.73/74. *(Note 14.10.2026: earlier text said the High Courts were split and one SCN per year was required.)*
8. **s.74 grounds** that don't meet *G.R. Infra Projects* (SC, 19.08.2026) (S1, S3, S4, S9, S10).
9. **Citation slips:** omitted or old provisions (s.42, old s.41 and s.16(4)), wrong rules and wrong notification numbers.
10. **DIN not printed** on notices served outside the portal (S7, S8, S9, S10). *Note 14.10.2026: a notice or order without a DIN or RFN is invalid (Gudla Vanaja, Telangana HC Full Bench, 30.09.2026). Check at once whether each has a portal RFN; if not, re-issue where the time limit allows.*
