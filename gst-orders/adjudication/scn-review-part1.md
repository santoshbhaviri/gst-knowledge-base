# Review of show cause notices S1 to S6

**Prepared:** 14 Oct 2026, for the Assistant Commissioner, CGST, Sangareddy Division.
**Covered:** S1, S2, S3, S4, S5 and S6. S7 to S11 are reviewed in a separate part.
**Standard used:** `gst-law/adjudication/03-court-proof-checklist.md` (44 SCN checks; grounds A to H), `01-adjudication-procedure.md` (limitation table, proper-officer limits), `02-appeals-review-litigation.md`, and the Act and Rules text in `gst-law/text/`. Circular and notification numbers were checked against `gst-law/data/circulars.csv` and `notifications.csv`.

**Confidentiality.** SCNs are named only by label. No taxpayer, supplier, officer, GSTIN, DIN or taxpayer-linked amount appears here. Amounts are given only as **small** (under Rs 10 lakh), **medium** (Rs 10 lakh to Rs 1 crore) or **large** (over Rs 1 crore), or as percentages.

**How each SCN was checked**
- Every page was read. S3 is a scan with no text layer, so its page images were read.
- The digital signature on each PDF was checked with `pdfsig`.
- Limitation was worked out from the annual-return due date (r.80) and s.73(2)/(10), s.74(2)/(10) and s.74A(2), using the table in `01-adjudication-procedure.md` §1.2.
- Key figures were recomputed (listed under each SCN).
- Defects are tagged with the checklist ground code (A1 to H9). They are ranked by how likely an appellate authority, the GSTAT or the High Court is to set the demand aside on that point.

**Issue(s) and period.** Audit SCN covering FY 2021-22 to FY 2024-25. It has eight paras:
- 3.1 GSTR-2A/2B vs 3B excess ITC, FY 2022-23 and 2023-24 (about 92% of the tax proposed);

- 3.8 interest on IGST of March 2023 paid in April 2023.

**Section invoked and whether right.**
- **Right:** s.73 for 3.1 (FY 2022-23 and 2023-24) and s.74A for the FY 2024-25 paras.
- **Wrong or doubtful:**

**Strengths (keep in templates)**

## S4

**Issues and period.** An **unregistered** mining lessee (identified by PAN), FY 2020-21 to 2022-23. Data came from the DG Audit (Central) performance audit.
- Issue 1: GST at 5% on sales of limestone slabs (about 98.5% of the demand).
- Issue 2: GST at 18% under RCM on royalty, DMF, SMET and permit fee (about 1.5%).

**Section and whether right.**
- s.74 is the only route open for FY 2020-21 and 2021-22.
- For an unregistered person, s.63 (assessment of unregistered persons) is the specific provision. It is not mentioned. Courts have allowed s.73/74 against unregistered persons, but the SCN should say why it chose s.74.
- Penalty is proposed under s.74(1) **and** s.122(1)(xi) "equivalent to the amounts demanded". Para 10 says "s.74(1) read with s.122(2)(b)". The clauses are inconsistent, and the double penalty is open to a s.75(13) challenge.

**Score: 3.0/10.** The forward-charge valuation appears inverted, so the main demand is very likely overstated. It rests on another wing's data that is not supplied, and no s.15 transaction value is established.

**Strengths**
- Clear subject line stating the section.
- Year-wise tables.
- Records two written requests for documents (28.11.2025 and 24.02.2026) and non-response. This is a genuine Explanation 2 "failure to furnish information on being asked in writing" fact, and the SCN uses it in para 9.
- Classification reasoning (heading 2515, Schedule I entry) and the RCM entry (Notification 13/2017-CT(R) S.No.5) are stated.
- s.74(5) and (8) options explained.
- DSC-signed. eOffice number present.

**Defects, ranked**

| # | Defect | Ground | Fix |

### Drafting rules these SCNs suggest

1. **One SCN per financial year** (safer practice: separate limitation per year and cleaner orders; not a legal requirement in Telangana, where a composite s.74 SCN was upheld in *Goldstone Infra*, 27.01.2026). Never combine s.73/74 with s.74A. Add a limitation box on page 1:
   - "FY ___; annual return due ___; s.73(10)/74(10) order deadline ___ (or s.74A(2) 42-month date ___); this SCN issued on ___, ___ days before the s.73(2)/74(2) cut-off."
2. **Tax period decides the FY**, not the payment date. Before signing, drop any item whose year is out of time (S1 para 3.5).
3. **Penalty line, fixed wording:**
   - "Penalty under s.73(9) [10% or Rs 10,000, whichever is higher]"
   - or "s.74(1) [equal to tax]"
   - or "s.74A(5)(i)/(ii)".
   - Do not add s.122(2). Use s.122(1)(...) only for a different act, with the s.75(13) reasoning.
4. **Interest line, fixed wording:**
   - "Interest under s.50(1) from ___ to date of payment, Rs ___ up to the SCN date"
   - or "s.50(3) for the period the electronic credit ledger fell below the disputed ITC (Annexure __)".
   - Apply the s.50(1) proviso (cash portion) where tax was declared in a later return.
5. **Hearing paragraph, mandatory:**
   - "A personal hearing is fixed on ___ at ___ (venue / video link) before the Adjudicating Authority. A separate notice will follow if this date is changed."
   - Delete "construed that they do not wish to be heard".
6. **RUD annexure, mandatory:**
   - number every document;
   - attach every working sheet, invoice list and third-party report (DG Audit, alerts, toll data, cancellation orders);
   - "available with the taxpayer" is allowed only for the taxpayer's own returns.
7. **s.74 / s.74A(5)(ii) heading** with (a) the specific non-declaration or failure to furnish on written demand (Explanation 2), (b) the evidence with RUD numbers, (c) the facts showing intent.
   - Pick one ground.
   - Never invoke it for a 2A/3B gap, a blocked-credit claim shown in the return, or an issue under litigation.
8. **2A/2B cases:**
   - state 2A or 2B, and the download date;
   - adjust for year-boundary claims (GSTR-9 Tables 8C/13) and credit notes;
   - annex a supplier and invoice list;
   - from 01.01.2022 rest on s.16(2)(aa)/rule 36(4), not on a presumption about s.16(2)(c).
9. **Valuation from secondary data:**
   - show the unit-conversion logic and a plausibility check (price per unit against royalty, against market);
   - establish s.15 transaction value or apply the valuation rules;
   - prove that turnover crossed the registration threshold year by year.
10. **Self-assessed tax (GSTR-1 > 3B):** cite the s.75(12) Explanation; state that s.73(11) penalty applies despite s.73(8).
11. **Late fee:** keep it out of the s.73 demand. State the machinery and the Notification 20/2021-CT band (preceding-year turnover).
12. **Standard paragraphs in every SCN:**
   - proper-officer authority (Circular 31/05/2018 as amended by 169/01/2022 and 254/11/2025; monetary band);
   - State-proceedings check (s.6(2)(b));
   - DRC-01A / ASMT-10 / spot memo dates and reply dealt with;
   - service modes (portal RFN + e-mail + speed post);
   - DIN or eOffice issue number (Circulars 122/41/2019, 128/47/2019, 249/06/2025, 252/09/2025).
13. **DIN/RFN and signature:** every SCN must carry a DIN/RFN; without one it is invalid (*Gudla Vanaja*, Telangana HC Full Bench, 30.09.2026). DSC-signing the SCN and every annexure remains good practice; open the uploaded portal copy to check. Do not upload scans of wet-signed notices (S3).
14. **Date:** "Dated: as e-signed", or sign on the typed date (S5).
15. **Corrigendum before the reply date** for clerical errors (S1 para 3.6, S4 citations). Only reductions are safe once the issue deadline has passed.
