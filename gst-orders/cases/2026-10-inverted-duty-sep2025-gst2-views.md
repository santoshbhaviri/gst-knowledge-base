# Inverted-duty refund for September 2025, across the GST 2.0 rate change: views

Case: an inverted-duty claim (section 54(3)(ii)) for September 2025, filed in August 2026. The RFD-08 was issued in September 2026 and the reply came in October 2026. The papers were read in full on 12.10.2026: computation sheet, extension request, reply, RFD-08, GSTR-3B, GSTR-1, the office's working sheet and RFD-01. The applicant is not named here and no figures are copied; the figures are in the case file.

## 1. The core question: whole month or only 22–30 September?

**Whole month. The notice is right on this point, and the applicant now accepts it.**
- A refund under section 54(3) is of credit unutilised "at the end of any tax period", and the tax period is the month.
- Under rule 89(5), "relevant period" has the meaning in rule 89(4)(F): the period for which the claim is filed. Turnover, Adjusted Total Turnover, Net ITC, ITC on inputs and input services, and tax payable must therefore all be for the whole of September.
- No provision creates a split period because rates changed on 22.09.2025 (Notifications 9/2025 and 10/2025-CT(Rate)).

**The "catch" about claiming the whole month's inputs is handled by the formula itself.**
- Net ITC (all inputs of the month, before and after 22.09) is multiplied by turnover of inverted-rated supply ÷ Adjusted Total Turnover.
- Here the inverted-rated (5%) turnover is only about a fifth of the month's Adjusted Total Turnover. So only about a fifth of the month's input credit enters the first part of the formula.
- Inputs used for the 18% supplies, before or after 22.09, are apportioned out automatically.
- Taking the whole month's inputs is not an over-claim in itself. It is what rule 89(5) requires.

## 2. Defects in the RFD-08 computation (important before passing the order)

1. **Mixed periods.** The notice took turnover and Adjusted Total Turnover for the **whole month** (from GSTR-1 / GSTR-3B), but Net ITC and ITC on inputs and input services from the applicant's Annexure-B, which covers **only the part-month**. Combining a whole-month denominator with part-month credit is what produced the negative figure. On consistent whole-month figures, the formula gives a positive amount larger than the claim. I recomputed the applicant's revised working and the arithmetic is correct. An order rejecting the whole claim on the "negative refund" ground would not survive appeal, because the notice itself breached the "same relevant period" rule it relies on.
2. **Reply time.** The notice gave **7 days** to reply. Rule 92(3) requires **fifteen days** from receipt. The extension already sought (to 14.10.2026) cures this in practice. Record in the order that more than fifteen days were allowed, and use 15 days in future notices (template N1 already does).
3. **The section 17(5) ground** (a small amount in the part-month Annexure-B) is valid, but it must be applied to the whole-month credit. Re-check the whole month's ITC register for blocked credit; the part-month check is not enough.

## 3. Can the higher revised amount be sanctioned in this order?

**No. Only the amount claimed can be sanctioned. The difference needs a fresh application.**
- Under rule 89(3), the credit ledger is debited "by an amount equal to the refund so claimed". The RFD-06 decides that application and cannot pay more than was claimed and debited.
- The applicant can file a fresh RFD-01 for September 2025 for the difference. Limitation under Explanation 2(e) runs from the due date of the September 2025 GSTR-3B; Notification 17/2025-CT extended a GSTR-3B due date, so confirm the date. That leaves time until about October 2027.
- The applicant's case law points the same way: allowing a differential claim on merits, not enlarging the original one:
  - Gujarat HC, Shree Renuka Sugars;
  - Bombay HC, Valmet Flow Control;
  - Delhi HC, Shri Shyam Footwear (the reply must be considered).
  - I have not verified these citations; obtain copies before relying on or distinguishing them.
- Say this in the order in one paragraph. The applicant then has a clear route, and the order is not open to the charge of ignoring the reply.
