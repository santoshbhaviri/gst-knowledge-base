# Template A3: Intimation before a show cause notice (Part A of FORM GST DRC-01A, rule 142(1A)), with the statement of ascertained liability

> **How to use this template.** Part A of FORM GST DRC-01A is generated on the portal; this text is the statement attached to it, showing how the liability was ascertained. Rule 142(1A) says the proper officer "may" send it (the word "shall" was replaced by "may" by Notification No. 79/2020-Central Tax dated 15.10.2020), so it is optional; it is recommended in every case under section 74 or section 74A(5)(ii), because the reduced penalty of fifteen per cent before the notice (sections 74(5) and 74A(9)(i)) helps only a taxpayer who knows your figures. Keep one of **[S.73] / [S.74] / [S.74A(i)] / [S.74A(ii)]**. Give a fixed reply date, diarise it, and **do not issue the show cause notice (A1) before that date has passed**. When the reply comes, answer it point by point in Part 3 of A1. Delete every "Drafting note" before issue.

> **Drafting note.** (1) Proper officer for rule 142(1A): Additional or Joint Commissioner, Deputy or Assistant Commissioner, or Superintendent (Circular No. 254/11/2025-GST, Table I). (2) Use the same financial year, section and figures you intend to use in A1, so that the notice can show what changed and why. (3) Allow a reasonable time, normally thirty days; an SCN issued before the reply time ran out has been set aside (*Varian Medical Systems*, Delhi High Court, 2025, persuasive). (4) Check that the reply date leaves enough time before the last date for the notice under section 73(2), 74(2) or 74A(2) (A1, Part 4); if it does not, go straight to A1 and record in A1 para 1.3 why DRC-01A was not issued. (5) Where the taxpayer pays in full, issue Part C and close the file; no notice follows for the amount paid. (6) Do not take a payment during a search or under pressure; record the voluntary nature of any DRC-03 paid after this intimation.
