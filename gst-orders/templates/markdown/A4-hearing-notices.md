# Template A4: Personal hearing notice, adjournment and final-opportunity notices, and record of personal hearing (section 75(4) and (5))

> **How to use this template.** This file has three documents. **(a)** the personal hearing notice, issued after the reply date in the show cause notice (or on any request); **(b)** the notice granting or refusing an adjournment, or giving a further or final opportunity, which records the count of adjournments under section 75(5); **(c)** the record (minutes) of the hearing, signed by the officer and by the person who appears. Print (b) and (c) on the letterhead as on page 1. Each notice is a separate, signed communication served on the portal (against the SCN, with an RFN) **and** by e-mail to the registered address, and by speed post where the noticee has not responded. A reminder that gives no date, time and place is not a hearing notice. Delete every "Drafting note" before issue.

> **Drafting note (b).** (1) Section 75(5): time is granted only for sufficient cause, with reasons recorded in writing, and not more than three times to a person during the proceedings. Count only adjournments granted at the noticee's request; record a date given by the office on its own (after a non-appearance without request) as a "further opportunity". (2) Before deciding ex parte, give at least one further dated opportunity after a non-appearance, served by portal **and** e-mail or post. (3) After the third adjournment, record that the statutory limit is reached and fix a final date. (4) Check the order deadline (A1 Part 4) before granting time; record any refusal that is forced by the deadline, with the date.
