# Template B3: Order-in-Original passed ex parte (no reply and no appearance despite three opportunities)

> **How to use this template.** Use it only where the noticee has filed no reply and has not appeared, after the SCN and **three separate hearing notices**, each served on the portal **and** by e-mail or post. An ex parte order is still a full speaking order: test every allegation against the evidence, apply any law that favours the noticee even though it was not argued, and confirm only what the record proves. Text in [square brackets] is to be filled in or chosen; delete every "Drafting note" before signing. Paragraph numbers follow template B1 so that the B2 finding modules can be dropped in.

> **Drafting note:** courts set aside ex parte orders where the SCN or hearing notices sat only in the portal's "Additional Notices and Orders" tab (*ACE Cardiopathy*, Delhi HC, persuasive); the Telangana HC relegates such cases to appeal with condonation of delay (*Bollant Industries*, *Sri Sai Ram Traders*, binding in Telangana). Serve by portal **and** e-mail **and** speed post, and keep the postal tracking print-outs on file. If the noticee is reported non-existent, use affixing or publication too. A hearing must be offered even if none was asked for (section 75(4); *B.K. Steels*, Telangana HC, binding). Never write that the hearing was "deemed waived" or that the allegations are "deemed admitted". If service of any notice cannot be proved, issue a fresh one before deciding.
