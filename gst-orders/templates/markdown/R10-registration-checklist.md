# Checklist R10: Registration checklist, one page per stage (new application, verification, cancellation and suspension, revocation)

> **How to use this checklist.** Print the stage you need and keep it on the file. Tick each box only when the step is done and the proof is on the file; write "NA" beside a line that does not apply. An unticked line points to the provision or case that explains the risk. Built from `gst-law/registration/01-registration-procedure.md` and `02-court-proof-checklist.md`. Cases are marked SC (binding on all), TG (Telangana High Court, binding here) or P (persuasive). Read the judgment before quoting it. The proper officer for all of this is the **Range Superintendent** (Circular 1/1/2017 as amended by Circular 223/17/2024-GST); the AC approves pre-grant verification under rule 9(1) proviso (b) and REG-03 requests for documents outside the REG-01 list.

**Reference: time limits**

| Step | Limit | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Approve a clean application | 7 working days (30 days if risky, no Aadhaar, or verification approved by the AC) | 9(1) |
| REG-03 on a new application | 7 working days (30 days in rule 9(1) proviso cases) | 9(2) |
| REG-03 on an amendment | 15 working days of the REG-14 | 19(2) |
| Decide after the REG-04 reply | 7 working days, or deemed approved | 9(3)-(5), 19(5) |
| REG-30 upload | 5 working days before day 30 (pre-grant); 15 working days after visit (post-grant) | 25 |
| REG-17 reply | 7 working days from service | 22(1) |
| REG-19 | 30 days from the reply or the REG-16 | 22(3) |
| REG-31 reply | 30 days | 21A(2A) |
| REG-21 | 90 days from service of REG-19, plus up to 180 days by the Commissioner / authorised Addl. or Joint Commissioner | 23(1) |
| REG-22 / REG-05 on revocation | 30 days from the application / from the REG-24 reply | 23(2), (4) |
| Gap-period returns after revocation | 30 days from the REG-22 | 23(1) provisos |
| Appeal (taxpayer) | 3 months + 1 month, to an officer not below Joint Commissioner (Appeals) | s.107; rule 109A(1)(b) |
