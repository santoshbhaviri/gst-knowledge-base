# Template R2: Physical verification report of the place of business (annexure to FORM GST REG-30, rule 25)

> **How to use this template.** Fill it in at the premises, or the same day, and upload it with the photographs in FORM GST REG-30 on the back office. Time limits (rule 25): **before grant**, at least **five working days before** the thirty-day limit in rule 9(1); **after grant**, within **fifteen working days** of the visit. Courts set aside cancellations based on reports that are undated, rely on unnamed neighbours, or do not say what was seen (Delhi HC *Umesh*, W.P.(C) 831/2024; Kerala HC *F R Trade Links*, 2021; see `/mnt/project-files/gst-law/registration/02-court-proof-checklist.md`, grounds R-D1 to R-D3). This report is the evidence the REG-17 and REG-19 will rest on, so write facts, not conclusions, and attach it to any REG-17 that relies on it. Delete every "Drafting note" before upload.
