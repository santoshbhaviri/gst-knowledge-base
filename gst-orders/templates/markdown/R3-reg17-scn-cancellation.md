# Template R3: Show cause notice for cancellation of registration (FORM GST REG-17, rule 22(1)), with ground modules and suspension

> **How to use this template.** The portal REG-17 has only a few lines for reasons. Put a one-line summary of each ground there ("Ground 1: rule 21(a), no business at the declared place on verification dated [ ]; see annexed notice") and attach **this notice**, signed, as the "supportive document" (line inserted in REG-17 by Notification No. 26/2022-CT). The Telangana High Court sets aside REG-17s that only repeat the words of section 29(2) or rule 21 (*Rayees Metals*, W.P. 17400/2024; *A1 Adil Traders*, W.P. 4539/2025), and orders that cancel on a ground not in the notice (*JK Technology*, W.P. 21690/2026). So: (1) pick only the modules that apply (G1 to G9), and give the facts, dates, periods and documents for each; (2) propose any **retrospective date** here, with its reason (para 4); (3) suspend only if para 5 can be written truthfully; (4) fill in the hearing date. The reply period is **seven working days from service** (rule 22(1)). Issue as the Range Superintendent, the proper officer under Circular No. 1/1/2017-GST as amended by Circular No. 223/17/2024-GST. Delete every "Drafting note" and unused module before issue.

**1. Why this notice is issued**

1.1 On the basis of the information and records set out below, there is reason to believe that your registration is liable to be cancelled under section 29(2) of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 (the Act) on the ground(s) stated in para 2. You are given an opportunity to show cause, with documents, and to be heard, before any decision is taken (proviso to section 29(2)).

1.2 This notice proposes **cancellation of registration only**. Any tax, interest or penalty for any period is not decided by this notice and will be dealt with, if at all, in separate proceedings. Cancellation does not affect such liability (section 29(3)).

> **Drafting note (para 4).** A retrospective date must be proposed here and tied to an objective fact. Without it, courts move the date to the date of this notice or of suspension (Delhi HC *Aditya Polymers*, *Subhana Fashion*, *Ridhi Sidhi*; checklist ground R-F1). For G2 alone, a back date is rarely justified; prefer the date of the order or of this notice.

**5. Suspension of registration**

5.1 **[If suspending]** Pending the decision on this notice, your registration is **suspended with effect from [DD.MM.YYYY]** under rule 21A(2), read with the second proviso to section 29(2). The reason for suspending now, rather than waiting for the decision, is that [state it: e.g. the declared place of business does not exist and invoices continue to be issued on the GSTIN, passing input tax credit of about ₹[ ] a month to recipients; continuing the registration pending decision would let further credit be passed on].

5.2 During suspension you shall not make any taxable supply, that is, you shall not issue a tax invoice or charge tax; you need not furnish returns under section 39; and no refund under section 54 will be granted (rule 21A(3) and (3A)). The suspension ends when these proceedings are completed (rule 21A(4)). It may be revoked earlier if your reply is found satisfactory.

5.3 **[If not suspending]** Your registration is not suspended by this notice.

> **Drafting note (para 5).** Courts quash routine suspensions (Madras HC *El Tech Power System*, 2026; Telangana HC *Rayees Metals*). Do not suspend for G2, G5 or G6 alone unless revenue risk is shown. Record the fuller reasons on file in template R6 and fill the date in the note at the foot of the portal REG-17.
