# Template R6: Recorded reasons to believe for suspension of registration (rule 21A(2)), and record of examination of a reply to a system FORM GST REG-31 (rule 21A(2A))

> **How to use this template.** This is an **office note**, kept on the case file, not served on the taxpayer. **Part A** records the reasons to believe before suspending under rule 21A(2) with a REG-17 (template R3, para 5). The short reason in R3 para 5 must be taken from it. **Part B** records the examination of a reply to a system-generated FORM GST REG-31 (rule 21A(2A)) and the decision to drop (R5) or to cancel (R4). Courts quash suspensions that are automatic or have no stated basis (Madras HC *El Tech Power System*, W.P. 9579/2026; Telangana HC *Rayees Metals*, W.P. 17400/2024), and a suspension falls with a vague notice (*A1 Adil Traders*, W.P. 4539/2025). A dated note written before the suspension is the best answer if the suspension is challenged. Delete every "Drafting note" before signing.
