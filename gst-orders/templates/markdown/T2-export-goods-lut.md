**2. Legal provisions**

2.1 Under section 16(1)(a) of the IGST Act, export of goods is a zero-rated supply. Under section 16(3) (as substituted with effect from 01.10.2023), a registered person making zero-rated supply is eligible to claim refund of unutilised input tax credit on supply of goods without payment of integrated tax under bond or LUT, in accordance with section 54 of the CGST Act and the rules, subject to the conditions prescribed. Rule 96A of the CGST Rules prescribes the LUT. Under clause (i) of the first proviso to section 54(3) of the CGST Act, refund of unutilised input tax credit is allowed in the case of zero-rated supplies made without payment of tax.

2.2 The refund is computed under rule 89(4):

> **Refund Amount = (Turnover of zero-rated supply of goods + Turnover of zero-rated supply of services) × Net ITC ÷ Adjusted Total Turnover**

where "Net ITC" is the input tax credit availed on inputs and input services during the relevant period; "Turnover of zero-rated supply of goods" is the value of zero-rated supply of goods made during the relevant period without payment of tax under bond or LUT, or the value which is 1.5 times the value of like goods domestically supplied by the same or a similarly placed supplier, as declared by the supplier, whichever is less; and "Adjusted Total Turnover" is the sum of the turnover in the State as defined in section 2(112), excluding the turnover of services, and the turnover of zero-rated and non-zero-rated supply of services, excluding the value of exempt supplies other than zero-rated supplies, during the relevant period. Under the Explanation to rule 89(4), the value of goods exported is the Free on Board (FOB) value declared in the shipping bill or bill of export, or the value declared in the tax invoice, **whichever is less**.

2.3 Under the last proviso to section 54(3) ("Provided also that…"), no refund of input tax credit is allowed if the supplier avails of drawback in respect of central tax or claims refund of the integrated tax paid on such supplies. Drawback of customs duties alone does not bar the refund (para 40 of Circular No. 125/44/2019-GST). Under the second proviso to section 54(3), no refund of unutilised input tax credit is allowed where the goods exported out of India are subjected to export duty. [Drafting note: the Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024 omits that proviso (from a date yet to be notified) and inserts section 54(15) of the CGST Act and section 16(5) of the IGST Act to the same effect. 2.4 Under rule 89(2)(b), the application is accompanied by a statement containing the number and date of the shipping bills or bills of export and the number and date of the relevant export invoices. Realisation of export proceeds is **not** a condition for refund of ITC on export of goods (para 48 of Circular No. 125/44/2019-GST). 2.5 Under clause (a) of Explanation 2 to section 54, the relevant date for goods exported by sea or air is the date on which the ship or aircraft in which the goods are loaded leaves India; for goods exported by land, the date on which the goods pass the frontier; and for goods exported by post, the date of dispatch by the Post Office.
