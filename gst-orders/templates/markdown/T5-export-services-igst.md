**2. Legal provisions**

2.1 Under section 16(1)(a) of the IGST Act, export of services is a zero-rated supply. Under section 16(4) of the IGST Act (as substituted with effect from 01.10.2023) and Notification No. 01/2023-Integrated Tax dated 31.07.2023, as amended by Notification No. 05/2023-Integrated Tax dated 26.10.2023, all services may be exported on payment of integrated tax, and the supplier may claim refund of the tax so paid. The refund is claimed under section 54(1) of the CGST Act, read with section 20 of the IGST Act.

2.2 Under section 2(6) of the IGST Act, "export of services" means the supply of a service when (i) the supplier is located in India; (ii) the recipient is located outside India; (iii) the place of supply is outside India; (iv) the payment for the service has been received by the supplier in convertible foreign exchange or in Indian rupees wherever permitted by the Reserve Bank of India; and (v) the supplier and the recipient are not merely establishments of a distinct person in accordance with Explanation 1 to section 8. The place of supply is decided under section 13 of the IGST Act: the general rule in section 13(2) (location of the recipient) applies unless the service falls in sub-sections (3) to (13). Clause (b) of section 13(8), which made the location of the supplier the place of supply of intermediary services, was omitted by section 157 of the Finance Act, 2026, with effect from [date of commencement of section 157: the law text records only the date of the Act, 30.03.2026, and no separate notification has been seen; confirm before use]. The omission is prospective: for supplies before that date, the place of supply of intermediary services remains the location of the supplier (section 13(8)(b)), and whether a supply is an intermediary service is decided on the tests in Circular No. 159/15/2021-GST.

2.3 Under rule 89(2)(c), the application is accompanied by a statement containing the number and date of invoices and the relevant BRCs or FIRCs. Under clause (c) of Explanation 2 to section 54, the relevant date is the date of receipt of payment in convertible foreign exchange (or permitted Indian rupees), where the supply had been completed before the payment; or the date of issue of the invoice, where payment had been received in advance of the invoice. Under rule 96(9), refund of integrated tax paid on services exported is claimed in FORM GST RFD-01 and dealt with under rule 89; the shipping-bill route in rule 96 applies only to goods.

2.4 This is a refund of tax paid, not of input tax credit. The formula in rule 89(4) does not apply, and the eligibility of the applicant's input tax credit is not an issue in this claim.
