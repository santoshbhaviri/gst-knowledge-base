1.2 Under rule 90(1), an application for refund from the electronic cash ledger is acknowledged in FORM GST RFD-02 automatically on the portal, and the time in section 54(7) runs from the date of filing.

**2. Legal provisions**

2.1 Under section 49(6) of the CGST Act, the balance in the electronic cash ledger after payment of tax, interest, penalty, fee or any other amount payable under the Act or the rules may be refunded in accordance with section 54. Under the proviso to section 54(1), a registered person claiming refund of any balance in the electronic cash ledger under section 49(6) may claim it in the form and manner prescribed, which is FORM GST RFD-01 under rule 89(1).

2.2 Circular No. 166/22/2021-GST clarifies that (i) the time limit in section 54(1) does not apply to refund of excess balance in the electronic cash ledger (S. No. 1); (ii) no certificate or declaration under rule 89(2)(l) or (m) is required, as unjust enrichment does not apply (S. No. 2); and (iii) TDS and TCS credited to the electronic cash ledger under sections 51 and 52 are equivalent to cash deposited, and any amount left unutilised after discharge of tax and other dues can be refunded as excess balance (S. No. 3).

2.3 Under section 54(10), where the applicant has defaulted in furnishing any return or is required to pay any tax, interest or penalty that has not been stayed by the specified date, the proper officer may withhold the refund, or deduct the unpaid amount from it. This, and not rejection, is the way to deal with dues.

**3. Limitation**

3.1 In terms of S. No. 1 of Circular No. 166/22/2021-GST, the time limit in section 54(1) does not apply to this claim.
