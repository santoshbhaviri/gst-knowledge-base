## Adjudication templates: read me first

Five templates take a demand case from the first letter to the hearing. The order-in-original and its checklist are templates B1 and C2.

| Procedure | Template | Use it when |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-SCN | **A3** DRC-01A intimation | Optional; recommended in every fraud case. Let its reply time run out before issuing A1. |
| SCN | **A1** (this file) + **A2** issue modules | Any demand of tax, credit or erroneous refund under s.73, 74 or 74A, **one financial year** per notice (recommended practice; a composite multi-year s.74 notice is lawful in Telangana: *Goldstone Infra*, Telangana HC DB, 27.01.2026), served with DRC-01. Paste one A2 module per issue into Part 3. |
| Hearing | **A4** (a) hearing notice, (b) adjournment / final opportunity, (c) record of hearing | After the reply date or on any request. A1 also fixes a first date. |
| Correction | **A5** corrigendum / addendum | Clerical slip, wrong citation, change of authority, reduced demand, documents served late. Never to enlarge a demand after the time limit. |

Worked out on 14.10.2026 from rule 80; re-check for any later extension. **Seven rules that remove most defects found in the eleven notices reviewed in 2026.** (1) One notice, one year, one section; never mix s.73/74 with s.74A. (2) Write the limitation in the notice and send it at least a month before the last date. (3) Fix the hearing date, time and place; never "deemed waived". (4) Number and enclose every relied-upon document. (5) Quantify tax, interest (sub-section) and penalty (clause) issue by issue in the DRC-01 13-column layout, crediting pre-notice DRC-03. (6) Plead fraud only with specific facts on one ground. (7) Sign the notice and every annexure digitally; serve on the portal **and** by e-mail or post.

> **Drafting note (Part 4).** The tax period decides the financial year, not the date of payment or detection. Count months exactly ("at least three months prior" to 31.12.2026 means on or before 30.09.2026). Office rule: send an s.73 or s.74 notice at least one month before its last date, so a corrigendum is still possible. Under section 75(10), proceedings are deemed concluded if the order is not issued in time; diarise the order date the day the notice issues. In an s.74 case, prefer to issue the notice within the s.73 time limit as well (two years and nine months from the annual-return due date), so that the demand can still be re-determined under section 73 if fraud is not upheld (section 75(2); Circular No. 185/17/2022-GST). For s.74A, no notice is issued where the tax for the year is less than ₹1,000 (proviso to section 74A(1)). Before relying on an extended date, check the Supreme Court's status in SLP (C) 4240/2025. Never write that the Telangana HC upheld these notifications: in *Brunda Infra* it observed that a GST Council recommendation is a sine qua non and that ratification cannot cure its absence (paras 94-97), but decided the cases on the Supreme Court's Covid exclusion (15.03.2020 to 28.02.2022), so their validity was left academic. Where possible, rely on the un-extended date or the Covid exclusion instead.
