> **How to use this template.** Each module is a ready set of paragraphs for one issue. Paste the module into Part 3 of A1 as para 3.[n], fill the brackets, and renumber. Every module has the same blocks: **(a) allegation**, **(b) provisions**, **(c) evidence**, **(d) computation**, **(e) verify before issuing** (an office checklist: do not paste it into the notice) and, where relevant, **(f) facts for section 74 / 74A(5)(ii)** (paste into Part 5 of A1). Use the issue's figures in Part 6 (quantification), Part 7 (interest) and Part 8 (penalty) of A1. Delete every grey box before issue.

> **Worked check on a conversion (hypothetical figures; delete before issue).** A seigniorage schedule fixes ₹8 per square metre of slab and ₹100 per tonne. The ratio means that 1 tonne corresponds to 12.5 square metres, so 1 square metre is 1 ÷ 12.5 = 0.08 tonne. To turn 10,000 square metres into tonnes, **divide** by 12.5: 800 tonnes. At ₹2,000 per tonne the value is ₹16 lakh. Multiplying by 12.5 instead gives 1,25,000 tonnes and ₹25 crore, an overstatement of 12.5 × 12.5 = 156.25 times. Test every conversion two ways: write the units and see that they cancel, and compare the result with an independent figure such as the royalty actually paid.

(7) Service: the noticee has no portal access unless a temporary registration or identification number exists (rules 16 and 16A); serve by hand or speed post and e-mail, and record it. (8) Penalty: propose it under the demand section only; add section 122(1)(xi) only if you record why failure to register is a separate act from non-payment (section 75(13)).

### Module 10. Late fee and interest: what is and is not demanded in a section 73 / 74 / 74A notice

> **(a) The rule.** Sections 73, 74 and 74A provide for the determination of **tax** not paid or short paid, tax erroneously refunded, or **input tax credit** wrongly availed or utilised, with interest and penalty on that tax. **Late fee under section 47 is not tax** and is not demanded under those sections. **Interest under section 50** is proposed in a section 73 / 74 / 74A notice only as interest on the tax or credit demanded in that notice. Do not put a "Fee" amount in the DRC-01 table of an A1 notice, and do not write "late fee under section 73(1)".

**(b) Interest on self-assessed tax paid late (no dispute).** Where the noticee filed a return late and paid the tax with it, interest under section 50(1) is computed on the part of the tax paid by debiting the electronic cash ledger (proviso to section 50(1); rule 88B(1)), unless the return was filed after proceedings under section 73, 74 or 74A for that period began. Unpaid interest on self-assessed tax is recovered under section 79 (section 75(12)). **Procedure:** intimate the amount in FORM GST DRC-01D (rule 142B(1)), giving the month-wise computation; the person must pay within seven days; the intimation is treated as the notice for recovery (rule 142B(2)); if unpaid, recover under rules 143 onward (rule 142B(3)).

**(c) Interest disputed by the person.** Where the person disputes the interest (for example, the cash portion, the dates, or whether a payment was in time), give him the computation and an opportunity to reply and be heard before recovery, and record a reasoned decision. Where the interest is on tax or credit that is itself in dispute, propose the interest in the A1 notice with that tax (A1 Part 7). **(d) Late fee under section 47.** Late fee is payable at the rates in section 47(1) (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B and other returns) and section 47(2) (annual return), as reduced by the notifications for the return concerned: Notification No. 20/2021-Central Tax (GSTR-1), No. 19/2021-Central Tax (GSTR-3B), No. 07/2023-Central Tax (GSTR-9). **How to recover unpaid late fee.** No form in rule 142 or rule 142B is prescribed for late fee: DRC-01D covers only tax and interest. The safe course is a short written notice stating the return, due date (after any extension), filing date, days, rate, maximum and amount, with fifteen days to pay or object; a hearing if the person objects; and a reasoned order. The amount so determined is an amount payable under the Act and is recoverable under section 79. Section 125 is not imposed for the same late filing, because a late fee applies.

(3) The GSTR-3B usually cannot be filed without paying the late fee computed on the portal: check what was actually paid before proposing anything. (4) Interest on self-assessed tax that is not disputed goes by DRC-01D, not by an A1 notice. (5) Never add a section 125 penalty for a late return.
