Paragraphs marked **[Option A]**, **[Option B]** are alternatives: keep the one that fits and delete the rest. Every "Drafting note" box is guidance for the officer and must be deleted before signing. Do not paste paragraphs of the SCN into the order: summarise them and give the SCN paragraph number. Write every finding in your own words, after reading the reply. Use template B2 for the finding paragraphs on the ten common issues, B3 where the noticee has not replied or appeared, and the checklist C2 before signing. Sign the order on the date printed on it, with your digital signature, and upload it with the DRC-07 summary.
