> **How to use this template.** Use it where the Appellate Authority, the Appellate Tribunal or a court has held that a notice under section 74(1) is not sustainable because fraud, wilful misstatement or suppression of facts to evade tax was not established. The officer who adjudicated the section 74 SCN (or his successor in that post) re-determines the tax "as if" the notice had been issued under section 73(1). Only years for which the section 74 SCN was issued within the section 73 time limits survive (Circular No. 185/17/2022-GST). Follow the appellate direction to the letter and go no further. Text in [square brackets] is to be filled in or chosen; delete every "Drafting note" before signing.

**1. Authority to pass this order**

1.1 Under section 75(2), where an appellate authority, Tribunal or court concludes that a notice under section 74(1) is not sustainable because the charge of fraud, wilful misstatement or suppression of facts to evade tax is not established, the proper officer shall determine the tax payable as if the notice were issued under section 73(1). The proper officer for this re-determination is the officer who adjudicated the section 74 notice (para 6 of Circular No. 254/11/2025-GST). The original order was passed by [the undersigned / the [designation] of this Division, whose post the undersigned now holds].

1.2 Neither the first Appellate Authority nor the Tribunal itself confirms a section 73 demand in such a case; that is for the proper officer (*Sterling & Wilson Pvt Ltd v. Commissioner, Odisha*, GSTAT Principal Bench, 11.02.2026, APL/1/PB/2026).
