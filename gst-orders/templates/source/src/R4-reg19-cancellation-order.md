> **How to use this template.** Use **Option A** after a REG-17 (or a system REG-31), and **Option B** on the taxpayer's own application in REG-16. Attach this order to the portal REG-19 as the "supportive document" and copy the effective date and a one-line reason into the form. Time limit: **thirty days** from the REG-18 reply (or REG-31 reply), or from the REG-16 (rule 22(3); Circular No. 69/43/2018-GST para 5). What the courts check (Telangana HC, binding): the order decides only the ground proposed in the notice (*JK Technology*, W.P. 21690/2026); it reflects the true record of reply and hearing (*Sheeshmahal*, W.P. 8344/2023); it gives reasons of its own; and any retrospective date was proposed in the notice and is justified (Delhi HC line). For non-filing cases, check the portal on the day of the order: if all returns have been filed and dues paid, **drop the proceedings by REG-20 (template R5) instead** (proviso to rule 22(4)). Delete every "Drafting note" before issue.
