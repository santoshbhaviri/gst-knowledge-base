**2. Legal provisions**

2.1 Under section 2(6) of the IGST Act, "export of services" means the supply of a service when (i) the supplier is located in India; (ii) the recipient is located outside India; (iii) the place of supply is outside India; (iv) the payment for the service has been received by the supplier in convertible foreign exchange or in Indian rupees wherever permitted by the Reserve Bank of India; and (v) the supplier and the recipient are not merely establishments of a distinct person in accordance with Explanation 1 to section 8.

2.2 Where the supplier or the recipient is located outside India, the place of supply is decided under section 13 of the IGST Act. The general rule in section 13(2) is the location of the recipient. Sub-sections (3) to (7) and (10) to (12) of section 13 contain specific rules (for example, services performed on goods physically made available by the recipient, or services that need the physical presence of the recipient, under section 13(3)); section 13(8) makes the location of the supplier the place of supply for the services listed there; and section 13(13) allows the Government to notify the place of effective use and enjoyment. Clause (b) of section 13(8), which covered intermediary services, was omitted by section 157 of the Finance Act, 2026, with effect from [date of commencement of section 157: the law text records only the date of the Act, 30.03.2026, and no separate notification has been seen; confirm before use]. The omission is prospective: for supplies made before that date, the place of supply of intermediary services remains the location of the supplier, and whether a supply is an intermediary service is decided on the tests in Circular No. 159/15/2021-GST.

2.3 Under section 16(1)(a) of the IGST Act, export of services is a zero-rated supply, and under section 16(3) (as substituted with effect from 01.10.2023) the supplier may make it under LUT without payment of integrated tax and claim refund of unutilised input tax credit. The refund is computed under rule 89(4):

> **Refund Amount = (Turnover of zero-rated supply of goods + Turnover of zero-rated supply of services) × Net ITC ÷ Adjusted Total Turnover**

where "Turnover of zero-rated supply of services" is the aggregate of the payments received during the relevant period for zero-rated supply of services and zero-rated supply of services completed during the period for which payment had been received in advance in an earlier period, reduced by advances received for zero-rated supply of services not completed during the period (rule 89(4)(D)); "Net ITC" is credit availed on inputs and input services during the period; and "Adjusted Total Turnover" includes the turnover of zero-rated supply of services **determined in the same manner under clause (D)** and of non-zero-rated supply of services, excluding the value of exempt supplies other than zero-rated supplies (rule 89(4)(E)).

2.4 Under rule 89(2)(c), the application is accompanied by a statement containing the number and date of invoices and the relevant BRCs or FIRCs. Under clause (c) of Explanation 2 to section 54, the relevant date for services exported is the date of receipt of payment in convertible foreign exchange or in Indian rupees wherever permitted by the RBI, where the supply had been completed before the payment; or the date of issue of the invoice, where payment had been received in advance of the invoice.

2.5 Circular No. 161/17/2021-GST clarifies when a supplier in India and a recipient outside India are establishments of a distinct person. Circular No. 202/14/2023-GST clarifies that payment received in Indian rupees through a Special Rupee Vostro Account, as permitted by the RBI, satisfies section 2(6)(iv).
