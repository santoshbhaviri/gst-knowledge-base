**2. Legal provisions**

2.1 Under section 16(1)(b) of the IGST Act, as amended with effect from 01.10.2023, a supply of goods or services or both **for authorised operations** to a Special Economic Zone developer or a Special Economic Zone unit is a zero-rated supply. Under section 16(3) (as substituted with effect from 01.10.2023), such a supply may be made under LUT without payment of integrated tax, with refund of unutilised input tax credit under clause (i) of the first proviso to section 54(3) of the CGST Act. The LUT provisions of rule 96A(1) apply to such supplies by rule 96A(6).

2.2 Under the proviso to rule 89(1) relating to supplies to a Special Economic Zone (which rule 89(2)(d) and (e) still describe as the second proviso), in respect of supplies to an SEZ unit or developer, the application is to be filed by the supplier of goods after the goods have been admitted in full in the SEZ for authorised operations, as endorsed by the specified officer of the Zone; and by the supplier of services along with evidence of receipt of the services for authorised operations, as endorsed by the specified officer of the Zone. Rule 89(2)(d) and (e) require the corresponding statements of invoices with that endorsement. Under the Explanation to rule 89(1), "specified officer" means a specified officer or an authorised officer as defined in rule 2 of the SEZ Rules, 2006.

2.3 The refund is computed under rule 89(4):

> **Refund Amount = (Turnover of zero-rated supply of goods + Turnover of zero-rated supply of services) × Net ITC ÷ Adjusted Total Turnover**

with the meanings given in rule 89(4)(A) to (F). The Explanation to rule 89(4) (FOB or invoice value, whichever is less) governs only goods exported out of India, so for supplies of goods to an SEZ the invoice value is taken, subject to the limit of 1.5 times the value of like goods supplied domestically (rule 89(4)(C)). For supplies of services, the turnover is the payments received during the period, worked out under rule 89(4)(D), and the same value is used inside the Adjusted Total Turnover (rule 89(4)(E)(b)).

2.4 Under clause (ba) of Explanation 2 to section 54, the relevant date for zero-rated supply to an SEZ developer or unit, where refund is claimed of the input tax credit used in such supplies, is the due date for furnishing the return under section 39 in respect of such supplies.
