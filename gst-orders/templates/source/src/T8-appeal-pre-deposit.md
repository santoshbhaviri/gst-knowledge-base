**2. Legal provisions**

2.1 Under section 54(1) of the CGST Act, any person claiming refund of any tax and interest, if any, paid on such tax or any other amount paid by him, may apply within two years from the relevant date. Under clause (d) of Explanation 2 to section 54, where tax becomes refundable as a consequence of a judgment, decree, order or direction of the Appellate Authority, Appellate Tribunal or any court, the relevant date is the date of communication of that judgment, decree, order or direction. Under rule 89(2)(a), the application is accompanied by the reference number and a copy of the order resulting in the refund, or the reference number of the payment of the amount under section 107(6) or 112(8) claimed as refund.

2.2 Under section 54(8)(e), the refundable amount is paid to the applicant, instead of being credited to the Consumer Welfare Fund, if the applicant has not passed on the incidence of the tax and interest to any other person. Under rule 89(2)(l) and (m), the applicant furnishes a declaration (where the amount claimed does not exceed two lakh rupees) or a certificate from a Chartered Accountant or Cost Accountant (where it exceeds two lakh rupees) to that effect.

2.3 Under **section 115**, where an amount paid by the appellant under section 107(6) or section 112(8) is required to be refunded consequent to any order of the Appellate Authority or the Appellate Tribunal, interest at the rate specified under section 56 (six per cent under the main part, or nine per cent under its proviso: Notification No. 13/2017-Central Tax, as amended; the rate adopted and its basis are recorded in para 9.6) is payable on such refund **from the date of payment of the amount till the date of refund**. Under the proviso to section 56, where a refund arises from an order of an adjudicating authority, Appellate Authority, Appellate Tribunal or court which has attained finality and is not refunded within sixty days of the application, interest at nine per cent per annum (Notification No. 13/2017-Central Tax, as amended) is payable from the day after the sixty days until the date of refund.

2.4 Under rule 92(1A), where refund of an amount paid as tax (other than on zero-rated supplies or deemed exports) is sanctioned, the part debited in cash is paid in cash, proportionately, and the part debited from the electronic credit ledger is re-credited to that ledger by FORM GST PMT-03.
