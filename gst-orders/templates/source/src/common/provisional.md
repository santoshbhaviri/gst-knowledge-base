**5. Provisional refund**

5.1 {{PROV_FIRST}} Under rule 91(2), as substituted by Notification No. 13/2025-Central Tax dated 17.09.2025 with effect from 01.10.2025, the proper officer is to issue the provisional refund order in FORM GST RFD-04 within seven days of the acknowledgement, on the basis of the identification and evaluation of risk by the system. Under the first proviso to rule 91(2), the proper officer may, for reasons to be recorded in writing, decide that the applicant is not to be granted refund on a provisional basis. Instruction No. 06/2025-GST dated 03.10.2025 (paras 3.1 to 3.3) directs that ninety per cent of the amount claimed be sanctioned provisionally where the system categorises the application as "low-risk", without further scrutiny for that purpose; that applications not categorised as low-risk get no provisional refund and are scrutinised in detail; and that the first proviso to rule 91(2) be used sparingly, case by case, and not on presumptive grounds or merely because routine proceedings such as scrutiny have begun. Provisional refund is not available to the categories notified under section 54(6) by Notification No. 14/2025-Central Tax dated 17.09.2025, namely a person who has not undergone Aadhaar authentication under rule 10B, and a person engaged in the supply of areca nuts (0802 80), pan masala (2106 90 20), tobacco and manufactured tobacco substitutes (chapter 24) or essential oils (3301); nor, under rule 91(1), to a person prosecuted, during the five years before the tax period of the claim, for an offence where the tax evaded exceeds two hundred and fifty lakh rupees.

Under para 3.2 of Instruction 06/2025, no amount can be adjusted or withheld under section 54(10) or 54(11) from a provisional refund; where the applicant has such dues, the Instruction allows the officer to decide the claim finally at the earliest instead, and recover the dues from the final sanction. Provisional refund may also be refused where, on an earlier refund of the applicant, the issue is pending in appeal, a show cause notice has been issued, or an order has not attained finality.
